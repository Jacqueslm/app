# The ad set

Written 12 Aug 2026. Every ad here is built from one observation, made from a
Google results page for "claude ai": **two small companies were sitting above
Anthropic's own listing, and both of them led with the same two words.**

> **Superhuman** — "No Credit Card Needed"
> **CREAO** — "Start Free, No Credit Card. Your first agent runs in about 60 seconds."

Neither out-ranked Claude. Both **paid** to sit above it, and both spent their
one line of copy on *free, no card* rather than on features. Against a bigger,
better-known name, that is the hook that gets the click — and it is exactly the
position this app now occupies.

---

## Before you spend a cent — read this

**Paid ads for drug and alcohol recovery are gated.** Meta, Google and
Microsoft all require **LegitScript certification** for US addiction services:
30–60 days to process, an annual fee, and not every business qualifies. Without
it, Facebook does not allow the ads in the US at all.

That gate splits this file in two:

- **Lane A — runnable now, no certification.** Binge eating and doomscrolling
  are not in the restricted category. 9,900 and 1,600 searches a month.
- **Lane B — written and waiting.** Everything alcohol- or porn-related. Do not
  submit these until certification is done, or you will collect disapprovals
  and a strike against the ad account.

**And nothing here runs before production access lands.** App-install campaigns
need a public listing.

---

## The structure every ad uses

Not the generic funnel. This one, which is what the two ads above actually did:

| Beat | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Recognition** | They see themselves. Not a claim, a mirror. | "It isn't hunger. You know it isn't hunger." |
| **2. The cost** | Name the thing nobody says out loud. | "It's the ten minutes after everyone goes to bed." |
| **3. Free, no card** | The differentiator, stated flatly. | "Free. No card. No trial." |
| **4. One action** | One verb. Never two. | "Start today." |

**Beat 3 is the whole point.** Every competitor in `KEYWORDS.md` charges —
Quittr is $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr. Leading with free is not a discount message,
it is the only structural advantage a solo founder has over a funded app.

## A4 · The switch ad — aimed at the competitor pages

This is the CREAO move, done honestly. They ran *"Beyond Claude · Switch From
Claude Today."* You have fourteen alternative pages already live and every
competitor on them charges.

**Static, 1080×1080:**

> **Paying $9.99 a month to not drink?**
>
> There's a free one.
>
> *FREE · NO CARD*

**Primary text:** Same counter. Same streak. Same lessons. A whole section for the person who loves you, which most of them don't have. Free — pay only if you want more.
**Destination:** the matching `-alternative` page, not the home page.

⚠️ **Only run this against competitors that actually charge** — Quittr,
Reframe, Covenant Eyes, Brainbuddy, Ever Accountable, Sunnyside. Several
others are free or freemium, and a false claim is both a policy violation and
the opposite of what this brand is.

## What to spend, and when

**Nothing yet.** In order:

1. **Production access lands** (~13 Aug). Nothing runs before this.
2. **Post the six scripts free.** They are the same hooks as these ads. Whichever holds average view duration past 9 seconds is the one to put money behind — you will have paid nothing to find out.
3. **Then $5/day on A1 only**, seven days, one ad, one audience. Watch cost per install, not impressions.
4. **Only then** decide whether LegitScript is worth it. It is a real annual cost, and Lane A may carry the whole thing without it.

**The mistake to avoid:** paying to distribute creative nobody has proven yet.
That is exactly how money goes out with no views coming back.
