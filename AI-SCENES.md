# AI SCENES — the videos that cost money

**This file is only the AI videos.** Talking-head scripts live in SCRIPTS.md.
Nothing is repeated between the two files.

Each one below is a complete video: every image prompt, every motion prompt,
every line you say, and the caption. Numbered A1–A5 so they never collide
with the numbers in SCRIPTS.md.

---

## HOW TO MAKE ONE (same 5 steps every time)

1. **Scenes tab** → paste the IMAGE prompt → generate the still.
2. **Animate** that still → paste the MOTION prompt → **5 seconds**.
3. Repeat for every shot in the video.
4. **Sequencer** → drop the clips in order → record the SAY lines over them.
5. Captions → reframe 9:16 → audio remaster → download → Buffer.

**Do this once, ever:** attach 2–6 reference photos of your man to every
Animate action, and the same for the woman. That's what makes it the same
face every time instead of random strangers — a series, not clips.

**Paste this at the end of every image prompt** (it's what keeps the look
consistent across all of them):

> cinematic photograph, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, moody realistic lighting, muted color grade, documentary realism, vertical 9:16 composition

**Money:** each image + each animate is a paid generation. A 5-shot video is
about 10 generations, roughly $2–2.50. Use **Draft or Standard** animate,
never "Best." Check your fal balance before a batch, and make them in one
sitting rather than one a day.

**Never show anything explicit.** The story is told by the glow, the closed
door, the posture, her face — never what's on a screen. Implied hits harder
and keeps you postable everywhere.

**How often:** about one AI video a week alongside your talking-heads. AI
stops the scroll; your face builds the trust. You need both.

**Don't buy a video subscription (checked Aug 2026).** Studio's "Standard"
animate already IS Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro — the same engine those $32/month
plans sell. Pay-per-use costs you about $0.44 a shot, so 5 videos a month
runs $13–26. A Kling subscription is $32.56 every month whether you make
anything or not. Break-even is roughly 6 minutes of animated video a month
— you're under it. If you ever go over, say so and we'll re-run the numbers.

**Where to post which:** A1 and A5 (the porn arc) carry the most suppression
risk — put those on YouTube first. Lean TikTok and Facebook on A2, A3, A4.
