# CARDS — SOMEONE + THEIR SUPPORTER (15, in the "Who catches you" style)

These match the card you uploaded: a dark navy gradient, the white logo up top,
**one big white line**, then **one yellow-orange turn line**, then `LINK IN BIO`
(yellow) and `TURN SOMEDAY INTO DAY ONE` (faint grey) at the foot.

**Why this exact format works** (from your own numbers): your "Who catches you"
card is the strongest thing you've shown. The big line names a feeling, then the
yellow line turns it into a gut-punch. That beat is what gets it saved and sent
to somebody — which is the behaviour that moves a small account.

**Layout, top to bottom:**

| Element | Style |
|---|---|
| Logo | white monogram, centered, small |
| Big line | white, bold, sans-serif, centered, upper third |
| Turn line | `#e5c158` yellow-orange, sans-serif, just below |
| Footer | `LINK IN BIO` in yellow, `TURN SOMEDAY INTO DAY ONE` in faint grey under it |

**Build it two ways, same as your partner cards:**
1. **Two-frame cut (better):** the big line alone for 4s → big line + yellow
   line for 6s → the `FREE TO USE / No card. No trial.` end frame for 3s. The
   cut in the middle is what makes the second line land.
2. **One still (faster):** post the full stacked card as a photo + an in-app
   sound. Use this when you're short on time, not by default.

**The caption must extend the card, never repeat it.**

**Hashtags (3–4, consistent):**
```
#addictionrecovery #lovinganaddict #recoverysupport #soberpartner
```
On the free/13-tracks cards, swap one for `#freeapp`.

**Order:** 1–5 (addict) → 6–10 (supporter) → 11–15 (paired). Post one a day,
not fifteen at once. Card 15 goes last — it's the one that puts the app (and
you) in it, and it lands hardest after everything before it was only about them.
