## The one thing to understand before sending anything

You said "reach out to mid-size to large companies about adding the app." That
instinct is right, and there is a faster version of it.

**A large company will not answer a cold email about benefits.** Boeing,
Centene, Ameren and Enterprise do not publish an address for their benefits
team, on purpose — everything goes through procurement, procurement wants a
vendor with a contract and a security review, and that is a six-month door for
a solo founder.

**But there are organisations whose entire job is handing employers a list of
recovery resources.** Get on one of those lists and you are in front of every
employer they serve at once. The national one, the RFW Institute, works with
**1,500+ employers across 35+ states**. One email there is worth fifty cold
emails to HR departments.

That is the same lesson the schools pile taught: the district office answers,
the individual principal doesn't.

So the pile runs in two lanes, and **Lane A goes first.**

## LANE D — the direct employers, your original idea

Not dead, but it needs a door. The St. Louis ones worth trying, all with
10,000+ local staff: **Boeing (Defense, St. Louis), Centene, Ameren,
Enterprise Mobility, Edward Jones, Anheuser-Busch, Emerson, World Wide
Technology, Schnucks, BJC HealthCare, SSM Health, Mercy, Washington
University.**

None of them publish a benefits address. Three real doors, in order:

1. **Get certified/listed in Lane A first**, then write to them as "a resource
   listed by the Recovery Friendly Workplace Institute." Completely different
   email.
2. **Somebody you already know** who works at one of them. One forwarded email
   from inside beats a hundred from outside. Ask around before Christmas parties
   season — you are in St. Louis, this is a small town for that.
3. **Their community foundation, not their HR.** Ameren, Enterprise and Edward
   Jones all fund local health causes and those inboxes *are* published. Worth a
   separate pass when the Lane A answer comes back.

I will build the Lane D list properly the moment Lane A gives you one yes to
lead with. Doing it before that is writing a hundred emails that get deleted.
