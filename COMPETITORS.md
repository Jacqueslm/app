# COMPETITORS — what's working, structurally

> **This file informs STRUCTURE, never copy.**
> You are here to see *how* a post is built — how it opens, how long it runs,
> what format it uses. Not what it says. Nothing in this file ever gets
> rewritten, reworded, or adapted into one of your videos.
>
> **Your own story is the content.** 38 years, free at 50, built the app you
> needed. Nobody else on this list can say that, which is the entire reason you
> win. The moment you start recreating somebody else's post, you throw away the
> only advantage you have.
>
> Take the shape. Fill it with your life.

---

## How to fill this in (30 minutes, once a month)

1. Copy the blank month block below and paste it at the top of the log, under a
   new dated header.
2. For each account, open their profile, sort by most-viewed, and record their
   **top 3 posts**.
3. For each post, fill in the **structure** columns only — never the script.
   - **Hook type:** question / bold claim / confession / "if you…" / list / stat
     / on-screen text / silence-then-talk
   - **Length:** seconds
   - **Format:** talking head / voiceover over b-roll / text-on-screen only /
     carousel / green screen / duet-style / photo montage
4. At the bottom of the month, write **one line**: the structural pattern you
   saw repeated. That single line is the only output that matters.
5. Hand that one line to me and I'll build it into the next scripts.

**Do not:** screenshot their captions, save their scripts, or paste their
wording anywhere. Structure only.

## Safety note

This is research on a computer, not time in the feeds. Open a profile, record
the numbers, close the tab. If a platform starts pulling you sideways, stop —
one month of missing data costs nothing. Your wife can do this pass instead if
it's easier; the columns don't require knowing the app.
