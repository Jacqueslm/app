# 50 a day — the outreach sheet

*Started 12 Aug 2026, because you said fifty a day in every category.*

## Read this once, then never again

**Every domain below is real.** They came out of Semrush ranking data or are
brands with national distribution — not from memory. That distinction matters
because I invented four YouTube channel ids once and it cost you a morning.

**What I cannot give you is fifty verified inboxes a day.** The machine I run on
is blocked from nearly every website, so I can find who to write to and where,
but I cannot open their contact page and read the address off it. So each row
gives you the **organization and the page**. Ninety seconds each: open it, find
Contact, paste the template.

**A contact form counts.** Most treatment centers, practices and brands publish
a form rather than an address. Filling it in is the same outreach.

**Do not send fifty identical emails.** Six templates below, each matched to a
category. Sending a rehab the influencer note is how you get ignored.

# How the next fifty gets built

Day 1 came out of six Semrush SERPs. **The same method regenerates it
indefinitely** and never needs me to guess a name:

1. Take a keyword from `KEYWORDS.md` or `TARGET-MARKET.md`.
2. Semrush `phrase_organic` → every domain ranking for it.
3. Throw out the ceiling — Mayo, Cleveland Clinic, NHS, SAMHSA, Healthline,
   psychiatry.org, MedlinePlus, Harvard. Unreachable and unbeatable.
4. What's left is small treatment centers, practices, one-person sites and
   non-profits. **All of them reachable, all with a blog, all needing free
   resources to point readers at.**
5. Match to a template by what kind of thing they are.

Twelve keywords not yet run through this: `codependency`, `al anon`,
`how to help an alcoholic`, `living with an alcoholic`, `porn addiction`,
`how to stop watching porn`, `phone addiction`, `how to stop doomscrolling`,
`sober curious`, `am i an alcoholic`, `binge eating disorder`,
`loving an addict`. **Each one is another fifty.**

# The rule that keeps this from wasting the effort

**Two follow-ups maximum, then let it go.** One week after no reply, once. Then
nothing. A third email converts nobody and burns the address.

**Log every send.** A name written to twice in a week is worse than one never
written to at all.
