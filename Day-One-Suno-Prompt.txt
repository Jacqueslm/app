SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL CLUB-RAP ANTHEM "DAY ONE"

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box)
Bouncy Jersey-club-influenced rap anthem at 138 BPM in C minor: syncopated
three-note kick pattern, hard snapping claps, ticking hi-hats and a deep
clean sub-bass that bounces rather than rumbles. Sparse minor-key piano
chords, one dark synth pad, and short vocal-chop stabs between phrases.
Chanting male lead, half-rapped and direct, with a room full of voices
answering every hook line and shouted "throw it up" ad-libs. Call-and-
response hook built on group chants, handclaps on the turnaround, one
stripped-back half-time bridge with drums and bass only, then the full
groove back for the last chorus. Punchy, wide club mix with the chant
sitting forward and the low end controlled, not distorted. Defiant,
grounded and clear-eyed — a victory lap, not a lecture. Original lyrics,
melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern; do not recreate or closely imitate
any existing song.

SHORT STYLE (use if the Style box rejects the long version)
Bouncy club rap anthem, 138 BPM, C minor, syncopated kick, hard claps,
ticking hats, deep bouncing sub bass, sparse minor piano, dark synth pad,
chanting male lead with a room full of voices answering, call-and-response
hook, handclaps, half-time drum-and-bass bridge, punchy wide club mix.
Defiant, grounded, celebratory. Original song.
