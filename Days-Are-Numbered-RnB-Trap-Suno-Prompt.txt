SUNO PROMPT — R&B TRAP WITH GOSPEL ELEMENTS
(pairs with the lyrics in Days-Are-Numbered-Suno-Prompt.txt)

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box)
Modern R&B trap with gospel roots, 140 BPM in B-flat minor, half-time feel
so the groove sits slow under fast hats. Tuned 808 with gliding slides
carrying the bassline, rolling triplet hi-hats, crisp finger-snap claps and
a hard, close snare; the drums stay sparse and let silence work. Warm
Fender Rhodes and a filtered grand-piano loop as the harmonic bed, a small
church organ that swells under every chorus, and wide, airy choir pads
stacked in the background from the first bar. Live tambourine and handclaps
on the turnarounds for the gospel feel, plus one real gospel choir answering
the lead in call-and-response on the hook and taking the bridge alone.
Close, breathy male R&B tenor — conversational and behind the beat in the
verses, full and melismatic on the hook, natural runs with only a touch of
pitch polish, double-tracked and harmony-stacked. Arrangement: organ-and-
choir intro with no drums, drums drop on verse one, 808 and hats carry it,
chorus opens up with choir and strings, verse two adds a counter-melody,
bridge strips to organ, piano and choir, then the last chorus brings
everything back and the outro fades on Rhodes and a hummed choir. Deep,
warm, slightly saturated mix with the vocal forward, the 808 controlled and
the cymbals soft; vinyl texture at the edges. Reverent and triumphant, a
church service with a trap drum kit. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence,
drum pattern and chord progression; do not recreate or closely imitate any
existing song or artist.
