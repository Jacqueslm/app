SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL GOSPEL-SOUL R&B "MY DAYS ARE NUMBERED"

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box)
Warm, gospel-tinged soul R&B ballad at 74 BPM in B-flat major, built on a
slow 6/8 shuffle. Fender Rhodes with gentle tremolo, upright bass walking
under the changes, live drums with a brushed snare and rim clicks, and a
Hammond organ swell that opens up under each chorus. Strings enter at the
second chorus and stay, with a small gospel choir answering the lead in the
final chorus and bridge. Close, breathy male soul tenor — conversational in
the verses, full and open on the chorus, with natural runs and no tuning
sheen — double-tracked on the hooks and harmony-stacked under the last
chorus. Sparse first verse (Rhodes, bass, brushes), a fuller second, a
stripped bridge of organ and choir, then the whole room on the out chorus
before it falls back to Rhodes and a spoken last line. Wide, warm analog
mix: vocal forward, bass round, cymbals soft. Celebratory and defiant
rather than mournful — a man celebrating a life, not mourning one.
Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and arrangement; do not recreate or
closely imitate any existing song or artist.

SHORT STYLE (use if the Style box rejects the long version)
Gospel-soul R&B ballad, 74 BPM, Bb major, 6/8 shuffle, Rhodes, upright bass,
brushed drums, Hammond organ, strings from the second chorus, gospel choir
answering, close breathy male soul tenor with runs, stripped bridge then
full out-chorus, warm analog mix. Celebratory, defiant, soulful. Original
song.
