## The five fixes (in order of importance)

### 1. Fix the first 3 seconds (the retention leak)
3s average watch time on Facebook means the hook gets the click but the first
three seconds lose them. The fix is the **two-frame cut** you already know from
the partner cards: big line alone → beat → second line lands. One still held
for 13 seconds is a poster people scroll past. Apply the two-frame cut to every
short, including the talking-heads (hard cut on the first punch line).

**Goal:** Facebook watch time from 3s → 5–6s. That single change roughly
doubles how many people the algorithm keeps showing you to.

### 2. Make the bio and the end CTA do one job
Reach that doesn't convert is usually a bio/CTA problem, not a content problem.
Right now every video says "Link in bio" — but the bio has to *finish* the job:

- One link, one destination: the app/site URL directly, not a linktree with
  three choices. Every extra tap is a drop-off.
- First line of the bio = the outcome, not the description. You already have
  this on Facebook: *"The habit isn't the problem — it's the symptom."* Keep
  that, it's strong.
- Second line = the offer: *free day counter + SOS tools + a section for the
  person who loves them.*
- End of **every** video: one specific instruction — "the 2-minute check-in"
  OR "the free app" OR "send this to her." One, not three.

**Goal:** followers, not views. 9 → 50 in a month is the realistic target with
the same reach.

### 3. Build for saves and shares, not likes
Your card format is built to be *sent to somebody*. That's the metric that
moves a small account (you already wrote this in the partner-cards file — it's
right). So end captions with an explicit save/share trigger:

- *"Save this for the hard nights."*
- *"Send this to the person holding someone up."*
- *"Save this. Day four is coming."*

Every video gets ONE of these. Saves and shares are what tells the algorithm
"show this to more people," and they cost nothing.

### 4. Double down on the two-sides angle (your search winner)
"Two sides change the story" is a search query people type into *your*
account. Nobody else makes content for both people in the house. That's the
moat. Post the new paired batch (SCRIPTS-ADDICT-SUPPORTER.md and
CARDS-ADDICT-SUPPORTER.md) in rotation:

- **Monday:** talking-head (addict or supporter)
- **Tuesday:** one card (from the 15)
- **Wednesday:** AI slideshow
- **Thursday:** one card
- **Friday:** AI movie
- **Saturday:** one card
- **Sunday:** rest, or re-post the week's best hook with a different title

Roughly **half your posts should be supporter/paired**, because that's the
half nobody else is serving and the half your search data says is pulling.

### 5. Borrow distribution with free features
- **Stitch the "myth vs truth" format** — the *"If they loved you, they'd just
  stop"* → *"Love isn't the lever"* pattern is already viral in recovery. Stitch
  one of your own myth-vs-truth answers onto it. Free, and it rides an existing
  wave.
- **Reply to every comment within an hour**, and pin the best one. 93
  engagements on 6,500 views means most viewers never talk to you — a reply
  turns a viewer into a follower.
- **Leave one genuine comment a day** on another recovery creator's post (not
  a link, just a real response). It's the cheapest audience borrow there is.

## The weekly rhythm (no spend, ~45 min/day)

**Daily (7 days):**
- Post 1 short (the rotation above).
- Reply to every comment within 60 minutes; pin the best.
- One genuine comment on someone else's recovery post.

**Tuesday (the 45-minute outreach block):**
- Send the next 5 emails from OUTREACH.md / OUTREACH-NEW-2026-08-18.md.
- Then 1 extra from the new piles: church OR workplace, alternating weeks.

**Friday (20-minute review, no posting):**
- Look at **followers, saves, and watch time** — not likes.
- Whichever video got the most *saves* this week, note its hook and make two
  more like it next week.
- If views climb and follows don't, it's the bio/CTA (fix #2). If watch time
  stays at 3s, it's the two-frame cut (fix #1).
