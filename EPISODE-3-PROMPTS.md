```
Seven vertical 9:16 images, 1080x1920, for episode 3 of a documentary-realism
short film. Generate them ONE AT A TIME, checking each against the reference
photo before starting the next.

THE MAN — identical in every image he appears in: Elias, a bald Black man in
his forties with a full dark beard, wearing a plain black t-shirt. Same face,

STYLE — apply to all seven: cinematic photograph, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of

vertical 9:16 composition.

NO CAPTIONS, NO SUBTITLES, NO TITLES, NO LOGOS, NO WATERMARKS, NO SIGNATURE.
Words are added later in editing. Three of these images contain handwriting
that is part of the scene — that handwriting is described in the shot and is
wanted. Nothing else on any image should carry text.

HANDWRITING RULE, for images 1, 6 and 7: it must be plain, legible, ordinary
ballpoint handwriting, the same hand in all three. Where a shot names the exact
words, those words must be readable and spelled correctly. Everything the shot
does NOT name should fall soft and out of focus rather than being invented as
unreadable scribble.

1. TWELVE DAYS
Close-up of an open notebook on a wooden table, shot from just above and
slightly to the side. Down the left of the page, a short column of handwritten
entries: "Day 1" on the top line, then "Day 2", and continuing in order down to
"Day 12" on the bottom line. Twelve lines, one number each, in order, all
readable. A pen resting beside the notebook. Warm afternoon light across the
paper. The top few lines are the sharpest; the lower ones may soften.

2. THE INVITATION
A phone lying face-up on a kitchen counter, seen from directly above, its

counter near it, not picking it up, fingers still. Evening light going blue at

3. HE GOES ANYWAY
Elias sitting at a crowded table in a warm, busy restaurant. Other people

4. THE WOBBLE
Extreme close-up of Elias's hand lying flat on a restaurant table, right beside

depth of field, the hand sharp and everything else gone.

5. OUTSIDE, ALONE
Elias standing alone on a pavement outside a lit restaurant at night, hands in
his pockets, his breath faintly visible in the cold. Behind him, through the

6. HOME
Close-up of a man's hand writing in a notebook on a wooden table, pen mid-

7. THE STACK
A stack of four identical closed notebooks on a wooden table, beside an open
```

## Check these when they come back

**Images 1, 6 and 7 are the handwriting ones, and handwriting is what these
tools get wrong.** Episode 2's "Day 2" — two characters — came back legible in
one version and upside down in another. Image 1 asks for twelve lines in order,
which is the hardest thing in the whole series.

**If image 1 comes back with garbled or out-of-order numbers**, don't keep
re-rolling it. Ask for this instead:

> Same shot, but tighter and at a steeper angle, so that only the top four
> lines — Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Day 4 — are sharp and legible, and the rest of
> the column falls away out of focus down the page.

Four correct numbers and a soft tail reads as a full page and removes the part
the generator can't do. Same trick works on image 7 if "Day 400" won't come.

**Image 3 — is he actually separate?** If he reads as part of the group, ask
again: nobody at the table looking at him, and only water in front of him.

**Image 4 — is the hand tense?** A relaxed hand is a different shot. It should
look like it is holding the table down.

**Image 5 — is there space above his head, and only one person on that
pavement?** Episode 2 nearly produced two Eliases off one word about a
reflection.
