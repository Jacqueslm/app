# KEYWORDS — the only source of search truth

Two live Semrush pulls (US database), both **6 Aug 2026**, plus later sweeps on
10, 12 and 15 Aug. **The subscription ended 15 Aug 2026** — nothing can be
re-pulled after that date. Every number here was verified against the live API before
saving; the two pulls agreed on every overlapping term. **Do not delete this
file. This file is final** — no re-running research, no fresh volumes, no
estimating numbers that aren't here.

Tags:
- **Build** — write for it (script, title, page, caption). The tag already
  weighs volume, difficulty, intent, and whether the app can honestly answer.
  A "(video)" note means social/YouTube first — the SERP is too hard for a page
  but nothing stops a face-to-camera video.
- **Too hard** — real demand, but a one-person site won't rank a page; no note
  means don't chase it in any format yet.
- **Skip** — wrong intent, someone else's brand, or something the app can't
  honestly claim. Volume never overrides a Skip.

Vol = US searches/month. KD = difficulty 0–100 (under 40 = winnable for a new
site). Blank KD = Semrush had no score (usually tiny volume).

## THE SEVEN TRACKED IN SEMRUSH (set 10 Aug 2026)

Position Tracking is capped at seven keywords on the free plan, so these seven
are the whole search scoreboard. They are all Lane 1, all difficulty **27 or
under**, and together they are about **1,420 searches a month**.

**Why these and not the obvious ones.** The first tracking run, set 8 Aug, used
broad terms and returned zero across the board — with samhsa.gov, Wikipedia and
drugs.com holding the top of every SERP. Those results are not hard, they are
closed: Google fills health queries with institutional sources, and a
one-person site does not displace a government agency. Two of the seven slots
were so broad they were pulling Shutterstock and Pornhub as "competitors",
which is a tracking slot doing nothing at all.

Nobody institutional writes for the person who *loves* an addict. That is why
Lane 1 is winnable and the head terms are not, and it is the same audience
outperforming everything else on TikTok and Facebook.

**Watch `is my husband an alcoholic` first** — difficulty 8 and the page is
already live.

**Do not check this weekly.** Search moves over six to twelve months. Set it,
record it here, and look again in the winter.

## THE COPY RULE (site-wide, every page, every video)

The app is for **everyone — straight, gay, bi, gender-nonconforming, every
gender** — and the supporter is not always a wife. So: headers say **"your
partner / the person you love"**; every supporter page names husband, wife,
boyfriend, girlfriend explicitly at least once so every reader sees themselves.
No competitor does this — every recovery app assumes a straight man. It's
lane #2 of the moat, and the cluster data below proves it's also free traffic.
