# Launch scripts — 13 talking heads, 7 to 15 seconds

Written 11 Aug 2026 for the shorts-only direction. Face to camera, no b-roll,
no music under the voice. Every title is a **verbatim search phrase** from
`KEYWORDS.md` with the real volume beside it — none of these are invented.

---

## The problem these are built to solve

You have **about seven seconds** of average watch across 2,271 YouTube views.
So the whole job is: the person who searched that exact phrase has to see
themselves in **frame one**, before they decide.

That's why every script below opens on the search phrase burned on screen and
the first spoken line lands inside three seconds.

---

## The five things, and where each one goes

You said what, who, why, how and where — in fifteen seconds. That's only
possible because **three of the five are never spoken.** Saying all five out
loud costs about eighty words and there's room for forty-five.

| | What it is | Where it lives | Words spent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WHO** | the person this is for | the title on screen + the first four words | ~4 |
| **WHY** | the line they recognise themselves in | beats 1 and 2, spoken | ~25 |
| **WHAT** | a free app | one clause, beat 3 | ~5 |
| **HOW** | one concrete thing it does | one clause, beat 3 | ~8 |
| **WHERE** | link in bio | the end card, silent | **0** |

**Never spend words on WHERE.** The end card does it. Never say "link in bio"
out loud — it's the single most skipped sentence on the internet.

---

## The shape — every script is three beats

| Beat | Seconds | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — hook** | 0 to 3 | The search phrase, said as a statement about them. No greeting. No name. Start mid-thought. |
| **2 — why** | 3 to 8 | The line nobody says out loud. This is the one that gets it saved. |
| **3 — solution** | 8 to 13 | Free app, one concrete thing it does. Once. Quietly. |
| **end card** | 13 to 15 | `99-free-end.png`, silent or music only. |

**45 spoken words = 15 seconds. 35 words = 12 seconds.** Word counts are on
every script below — if you rewrite one, keep it under 45 or it won't fit.

---

## How to shoot them

- **No "hey guys", no "so I want to talk about"**. First word is the hook. Every
  second before it is a second of your seven spent on nothing.
- **Down the lens, not at yourself.** Look at the small dot, not the screen.
- **Don't say the app's name until beat 3.** The moment it becomes an ad, they
  go. Twelve seconds of truth buys you three seconds of ask.
- **Burn the title on screen at 0:00** and paste the same phrase verbatim into
  the caption. On YouTube the title field is the search box; on TikTok the
  on-screen text and caption are. Same words in all three.
- **One take, one room, same shirt** across a batch. Shoot five in an hour.
- Don't smile at the end. These aren't good news.

## Posting order

**1, 3, 12, 9, 11** first — that's the two easiest partner terms, the biggest
winnable term, the biggest moat term, and the one where you tell your own story.
Five videos, one an hour to shoot, one a day to post.

Then the rest in any order. **5 and 10 are the two most likely to travel** and
also the two most likely to draw a nasty comment; post them once the first five
have settled.

Don't post a card and a talking head on the same day. One thing a day.

## What tells you it's working

Not views. **Average view duration.** These are 12 to 15 seconds long — if the
average lands at 9 or 10, the hook is doing its job and you keep going. If it
stays at 7, beat 1 is too slow and the fix is cutting words from the opening
line, not making a different video.
