# NEW SPACES — where this lands that we haven't touched

Researched 20 Aug 2026, live web, numbers cited to their source. Everything in
`OUTREACH.md` (rehabs), `SCHOOLS.md`, `COMPANIES.md` and `INFLUENCERS.md` is
already worked — 47/50, 22/22, 8/20, 10/33 sent. This file is the spaces those
four piles do not reach.

**Chosen on one test: is there a real, published number saying addiction is
worse in this group than in the general population?** Nothing here is a hunch.

---

## 1. BUILDING TRADES UNIONS — the highest-rate industry in America

**The number:** roughly **15% of US construction workers have a substance use
disorder against 8.6% of the general population** — nearly double, the highest
rate of any industry. Construction ranks first in marijuana use, heroin use and
pain-reliever misuse, with **16.5% heavy alcohol use** among full-time workers.
([SAMHSA/CBHSQ](https://www.samhsa.gov/data/sites/default/files/report_1959/ShortReport-1959.html) · [Carrum Health](https://carrumhealth.com/blog/construction-industry-substance-use/))

**Why they're reachable:** several unions already run Member Assistance
Programs and are actively looking for tools to hand members —
[SMART's MAP](https://www.smart-union.org/union-mental-health-programs-focus-on-smart-members-well-being/),
[Bricklayers' MAP](https://bacbenefits.org/member-assistance-program),
Operating Engineers Local 3's Assistance and Recovery Program, and the
Wisconsin Laborers' LEAN programme.
[Maine AFL-CIO trains union peer-support specialists](https://maineaflcio.org/news/finding-healing-through-solidarity-union-peer-support-specialists-help-fellow).

**Why us specifically:** a man on a site does not want an app his foreman can
see. Discrete mode changes the icon and name on his phone. And the free tier
means a hall can hand it out without a budget line or a procurement process.

**Targets:** MAP/EAP coordinators at SMART, BAC, IUOE locals, LIUNA district
councils, and state AFL-CIO peer-support programmes. Start with St. Louis and
Missouri locals — local beats national for a one-man operation.

---

## 2. FIRE, EMS AND POLICE PEER SUPPORT

**The number:** first responders are about **twice as likely** to develop a
substance use problem. Roughly **29% of firefighters misuse alcohol** and
**10% drugs**; about **35%** of those also carry PTSD, depression or anxiety
symptoms. ([AddictionHelp](https://www.addictionhelp.com/addiction/first-responders/) ·
[Serve & Protect](https://www.serveprotect.org/post/exploring-the-impact-substance-abuse-rates-among-first-responders))

**Why they're reachable:** the structure already exists — CISM teams,
department peer-support programmes, and state behavioural-health offices like
[Indiana DHS First Responder Behavioral Health](https://www.in.gov/dhs/fire-and-building-safety/first-responder-behavioral-health/).
The [FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin](https://leb.fbi.gov/articles/featured-articles/first-responder-peer-support-programs)
runs pieces on peer support, so the topic is not taboo any more.

**Why us specifically:** the whole barrier here is being seen asking. Nothing
in the app requires a name, a card or a referral, and the SOS tools work at
3am between calls. Say that and nothing else.

## 3. COLLEGIATE RECOVERY PROGRAMS — 183 already organised

**The number:** [ARHE has 183 institutional members](https://collegiaterecovery.org/about-arhe/),
each one a staffed collegiate recovery programme with students already
identified as in recovery. There is a
[public directory](https://collegiaterecovery.org/collegiate-recovery-directory/),
and membership enquiries go to **mack@collegiaterecovery.org**.

**Why they're reachable:** unlike a rehab, a CRP has no product to protect. A
free tool for their students is a straight win, and CRP directors talk to each
other constantly — one listing travels.

**Why us specifically:** students are broke. Every competitor charges. And this
is the age group most likely to be dealing with porn, gaming, vaping and
scrolling rather than the substances a campus counselling centre is set up for.

**This is the single best pile in this file** — organised, listed, free to
approach, and the audience is exactly who cannot pay.

## 4. RECOVERY FRIENDLY WORKPLACE — and Missouri runs one

**The number:** every state now has some version. Missouri's is real and local:
[Recovery Friendly Missouri](https://recoveryfriendlymo.com/), run with
[MU Extension and the Missouri Chamber](https://mochamber.com/news-archive/missouri-chamber-works-with-mu-extension-to-create-recovery-friendly-workplace-credential/),
SAMHSA-funded, giving employers free resources and a designation.

**Live contacts, published:** Chiquita Chanay **chanays@missouri.edu**,
Michelle McDowell **mcdowellm@missouri.edu**. There is an employer orientation
on **8 September 2026** ([Missouri SBDC](https://sbdc.missouri.edu/training/recovery-friendly-workplace-orientation-september-8-2026)).

**Why this one matters more than the rest:** it is a Missouri programme whose
entire job is putting free recovery resources in front of employers. You are a
Missouri founder with a free recovery app. Getting into their toolkit reaches
every designated employer in the state at once, and it costs them nothing to
add. The [DOL keeps a state-by-state list](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/RRW-hub/additional-resources/state-resources)
if it works and you want to repeat it elsewhere.

**Do this one first.**

## 5. STATE PROBLEM GAMBLING COUNCILS

**Why now:** gambling is the addiction with no smell and no tell, it is the
one you just made a video about, and almost no app serves it. Every state has
an affiliate under the
[NCPG](https://www.ncpgambling.org/about-us/state-affiliates/), each with a
helpline and a resources page.

**Note:** the Illinois council was already on the list as form-only. The other
affiliates are separate organisations with their own contacts — this is a pile,
not one target.

## 6. CHURCH RECOVERY MINISTRIES

**The number:** Celebrate Recovery runs in
[over 35,000 churches](https://celebraterecovery.com/about/), with more than
5 million people having completed a step study.

**Why us specifically:** the Spiritual Path is now woven into the daily lesson
for anyone who chooses faith — God as you understand God, no denomination, no
argument. That is exactly the register CR uses, and no other recovery app has
it. A CR ministry leader is a volunteer at a local church, which means no
procurement, no committee, and a straight yes or no.

**How:** individual ministry leaders, found through celebraterecovery.com's
group finder. Start with St. Louis. This is a slow, one-at-a-time pile — but
the fit is the closest of anything in this file.

## THE OBJECTIVE PART — what will and will not convert

Fifty rehab emails have gone out. The one reply so far pointed at a
partnership form. That is not a failure, it is what listing outreach does:
slow, low hit rate, occasionally one that pays for the whole year.

Ranked honestly by how likely they are to convert:

1. **Recovery Friendly Missouri** — one email, local, their literal job is
   distributing free resources. Highest chance of a yes in this file.
2. **Collegiate recovery programmes** — 183 of them, listed, no competing
   product, audience that cannot pay.
3. **Church recovery ministries** — best product fit, slowest process, no
   gatekeepers.
4. **Union MAPs** — highest-need audience in America, but a benefits
   coordinator moves slowly and may want a vendor agreement.
5. **First responder peer support** — real need, hardest to reach cold.
6. **Gambling councils** — small pile, good fit, mostly forms.

**And the uncomfortable one:** none of these six is faster than the thing you
are already doing. Twelve posts a day across three channels reaches more
people this week than fifty emails will this month. Outreach is the long game
that compounds; content is what converts now. Do both, but do not let the
email pile eat the hours that make videos.
