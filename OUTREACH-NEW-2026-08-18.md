# SECTION B — CHURCHES / FAITH RECOVERY (new category)

**The angle:** Celebrate Recovery and similar church recovery ministries run
weekly groups for "hurts, hang-ups, and habits." The leader of that group is a
natural ally — they already hand out resources every week, and their members'
families are exactly your supporter audience. You're not selling them anything;
you're offering a free tool to hand to the family members sitting in the lobby
while their loved one is in the meeting.

**The one rule:** keep it free, keep it private, never mention money. This is a
"here's a free thing for your families" note, not a pitch.

**The ask:** one mention from the group leader or a flyer/handout on the
resource table. That's it.

# SECTION C — JOBS / WORKPLACES (new category)

**The angle:** every large employer and union runs an **Employee Assistance
Program (EAP)** — the confidential benefit that covers mental health and
substance use. EAP coordinators are always looking for free, private, opt-in
resources to list. Your app is exactly that, and "confidential, nobody at work
sees anything" is the whole sales point.

**The rule is the same as schools:** free resource, opt-in, employee decides in
private. Never mention money — the moment pricing enters, procurement enters.

**What a "yes" looks like:** one line in an EAP newsletter or benefits portal
that reaches hundreds or thousands of employees. Not a contract.

### C-master — use this body, change the first line
**Subject:** A free, private recovery resource for your EAP

Hi,

I'm a St. Louis app builder and this will be short.

I built a private recovery-and-wellbeing app called Turn Someday Into Day One —
a day counter, SOS tools for the hard ten minutes, and 30-day programs across
13 tracks: alcohol, food, gambling, social media, anger and more.

Two things make it a fit for an EAP. First, it's completely private — nobody
sees anyone's data, not us, not the employer, not a spouse. Second, it has a
whole section for someone worried about a loved one — the second group in any
workforce is bigger than anyone says out loud.

I was addicted for 38 years. At 50 I got free and built what I'd needed. The
offer is simple: free to start, opt-in, nothing for your team to run. If you'd
consider a mention in an EAP email or benefits portal, I'll write the blurb
myself.

https://www.turnsomedayintodayone.com

Jacques
