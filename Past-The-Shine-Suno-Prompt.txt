SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL HIP-HOP "PAST THE SHINE"
Two separate prompts. The audio prompt goes in the Style box. The lyrics prompt goes in the Lyrics box. Never paste one into the other.

====================== AUDIO PROMPT — PASTE IN THE STYLE BOX (734 characters) ======================
Slow, spacious hip-hop, 82 BPM, minor key, straight 4/4 with a lazy swing.
Heavy 808 long and sustained, tuned low, kick and 808 riding together; sparse
trap hats, closed and rolling; filtered piano loop holding the spine, one muted
guitar figure answering it, dusty string pad under the chorus. Bridge drops to
sub-bass and one voice, nothing else. Close-miked low male vocal, almost spoken
in the verses, restrained on the hook, doubled an octave down; a female alto
layers the chant on the final chorus. Wide ambient room, cinematic and empty,
deep low end, no clutter, no bright sheen, one sustained note held under the
last chorus. Confident and understated, never boastful. Original lyrics,
melody and arrangement; no imitation.
