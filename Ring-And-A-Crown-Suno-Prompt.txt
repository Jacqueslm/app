SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL AFROBEATS-R&B "LADIES' NIGHT" ANTHEM

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box)
Warm Afrobeats and R&B crossover at 104 BPM in A major: bouncy log-drum groove, live shaker, soft congas and a rounded sub-bass that walks under the kick. Clean electric-guitar arpeggio, bright plucked synth, airy pads and a two-chord piano lift under the hook. Smooth male tenor lead, close and conversational, answered by a small female group singing "ooh" harmonies and light call-and-response chants. Hook built on a handclap pattern and a four-note bell melody; one short percussion break before the final chorus, then a soft keys-and-guitar outro. Glossy, modern, radio-ready mix with the vocal forward and the bass warm, not distorted. Romantic, celebratory, respectful and clean throughout, with a queens-and-crowns mood rather than anything crude. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
