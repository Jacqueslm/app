# SCHOOLS — the staff-wellness pile

**Same rules as OUTREACH.md.** Copy the subject, copy the body, paste, send,
tick the box. One a week, in the extra 15 minutes after Tuesday's five rehab
emails. No deadline on this pile.

**The one rule that matters: never mention money to a district.** Free
resource, opt-in, staff decide for themselves like any other app. The moment
pricing enters, procurement enters — W-9s, insurance certificates, board
approval, months. You're offering their newsletter a free item, not selling
them software. That's the whole trick, and it's why this works at all.

**What a "yes" actually looks like:** one paragraph in a staff wellness email
that reaches 500–5,000 people, at no cost. Not a contract. Then the next
district hears "we're already in [district]'s staff wellness resources."

Full strategy and the numbers behind it: `reference/SCHOOLS-BENEFITS-PLAYBOOK-2026-08.md`
