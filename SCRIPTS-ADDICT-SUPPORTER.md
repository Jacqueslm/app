# SCRIPTS — SOMEONE + THEIR SUPPORTER (20–25s, both sides)

This batch is different from SCRIPTS.md on purpose. Those are 15-second
gut-punches. **These are 20–25 seconds and each one answers all five questions —
WHAT, WHO, WHEN, WHERE, HOW** — because a stranger needs the full picture before
they tap. Hook lands first, in the first three seconds.

**Three categories, five each:**

| Category | Numbers | What it is | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| TALK | T1–T5 | You on camera, speaking | $0 |
| SLIDESHOW | S1–S5 | AI photo series + captions (like the Meg/Lily format) | paid stills |
| MOVIE | M1–M5 | AI animated scenes + voiceover | paid stills + animate |

**The through-line (this is why the batch works):** every video tells the story
from **two sides — the person struggling AND the person holding them up.** Your
own TikTok search data already shows it pulling: *"How do the two sides change
the story?"* is a top search query on your account. Nobody else makes content
for both people in the house. That is the moat. Lean on it.

**Research that shaped these (18 Aug 2026):**
- Sober TikTok/shorts travel on **myth-vs-truth turns** ("If they loved you,
  they'd just stop" → "Love isn't the lever") and on **naming one specific
  unspoken moment** (2am, the doorway, counting the bottles) rather than
  general advice.
- The **supporter lane is a documented gap**: creator after creator says
  "people see the addiction, they don't see the family." Your app is the only
  one that gives the family their own program. Say it out loud, every time.
- Your top performers already prove it: "You don't quit at noon" (427 views),
  "YOU MATTER" / "YOU ARE VALID TOO" (389/430) — direct-address + validating
  the supporter. These are built to repeat that pattern, not reinvent it.

**Every caption below already contains the WHAT/WHO/WHEN/WHERE/HOW.** Don't trim
them down to one line — the 5W+H caption is the whole point of this batch.

**Hashtags (same set every time, 3–4 max):**
