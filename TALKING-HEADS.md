# TALKING HEADS — 5 scripts (you on camera, 20–25s each, $0)

These are the five TALK scripts from the 18 Aug batch, pulled out into their own
downloadable file. Each one answers all five questions — **WHAT, WHO, WHEN,
WHERE, HOW** — in the caption, because a stranger needs the full picture before
they tap. The hook lands first, in the first three seconds.

**Record exactly like SCRIPTS.md:** one person, one camera, slow. Read it three
times, keep the third take. No AI here — your real face is the whole point.

**Hashtags (3–4 max, same set every time):**
```
#addictionrecovery #lovinganaddict #recoverysupport #soberpartner
```
On the free-app / 13-tracks lines, swap one for `#freeapp`.

## T1 — "Who catches you" · supporter · 22s

**YouTube title:** Who catches you when you're the one who falls
**TikTok / Facebook:** Who catches you when you're the one who falls?

**Hook (first line):** Who catches you when you're the one who falls?

**The situation — WHAT / WHO / WHEN / WHERE / HOW:**
- WHAT — The invisible second job of loving someone through addiction.
- WHO — The supporter: wife, husband, mother, father, son, daughter, best friend. (And the addict they're holding up.)
- WHEN — The worst ten minutes: 2am, after the relapse, after the lie.
- WHERE — The room next to them. Your own home, which stopped feeling safe.
- HOW — You can't pour from an empty cup. The app has a whole section built for YOU — free, private, not a footnote in his.

>
> So I built you one. Free. Your own day counter, your own SOS button,
> your own lessons — for the person who loves the addict.
>
> Because you can't pour from an empty cup.

**Caption:** WHO — the person holding up an addict: wife, husband, mom, son, best friend. WHAT — the invisible second recovery nobody names. WHEN — 2am, the worst ten minutes. WHERE — the room next to him. HOW — your own day counter, SOS tools, and lessons built for you, free. You're not the footnote in his story. Link in bio.
