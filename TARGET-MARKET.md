**The two words this audience actually types are `al anon` and `codependency`.**

- **codependency, 33,100/mo, CPC $0.12.** Same size, and **almost no
  advertiser competition at all.** Difficulty 72 is hard, but that CPC says
  nobody is monetizing this. That is a strange and interesting gap.

## So who is the target market

**Primary: the person living with somebody else's addiction.** Three times the
search demand of the drinker, they are the ones actively looking for help at 11pm,
and you are the only app with a section built for them. Al-Anon has existed since
1951 and nobody ever built them software.

**Secondary: the person with a hangover and a bad feeling.** Not "the alcoholic"
— someone who has not accepted that label and never will. That is what
`hangxiety` at 12,100 is telling you. They do not search "am i an alcoholic"
(3,600, difficulty 68). They search for the symptom.

**Third: doomscrolling and binge eating.** Both winnable, both need no
certification to advertise, and both reach people who would never call
themselves addicts.

**The copy rule still holds over all of it.** Titles are verbatim search phrases,
so `is my husband an alcoholic` stays exactly as typed. What is *said* never
assumes a husband and a wife. It is built for all types of couple.

## What I would do with this

1. **Two new pages: `/codependency` and `/al-anon`.** 66,200 searches a month
   between them and you have nothing pointed at either.
2. **Push hangxiety hardest.** Best volume-to-difficulty ratio you own, and no
   advertiser is fighting for it.
3. **Retitle the porn page** toward "how to stop watching porn" (8,100) instead
   of a 20-a-month phrase.
4. **Drop gambling as SEO.** Difficulty 94. Keep it as video and outreach.
5. **Doomscrolling is free money at difficulty 24.** No certification needed.

Your call on all five.
