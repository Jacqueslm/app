The best addiction recovery apps in 2026, compared honestly

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps below — ours. We still list the others honestly, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Most "best recovery apps" lists were written years ago and copied from each other since. This one is current, short, and honest about who each app is actually for.

The apps

I Am Sober

Best known all-rounder

The most popular sobriety tracker: day counter, milestones, daily pledges, and a large community. Covers many habits, not just alcohol.

Free basics · paid tier roughly $10/mo or $40/yr — check their site

Reframe

Best for cutting back on alcohol specifically

A structured, course-style program focused on drinking less or quitting, with daily readings and exercises. Alcohol only — if your struggle is something else, it isn't built for you.

Around $100/yr — check their site

Sunnyside

Best for mindful drinking (not quitting)

Tracking and gentle goals for people who want to drink less rather than stop. If you already know moderation hasn't worked for you, look elsewhere on this list.

Around $9/mo — check their site

Nomo

Best free option

Simple sobriety clocks — as many as you want, for anything you're quitting. No program, no lessons, but genuinely free and it does what it says.

Free

Sober Time

Solid tracker with community

Day counting, motivation, and a community feed. Similar territory to I Am Sober — try both and keep the one that feels right.

Free basics · small paid tier

Loosid

Best for sober social life

Less a recovery program, more a sober social network — events, dating, community. Strong once you're steady and want a life that doesn't revolve around avoiding things.

Free basics · paid tiers — check their site

The gap: every single one is built for the person struggling

Look down that list again. Day counters for him. Programs for him. Communities for him.

Nothing for the person lying awake next to him.

The wife who found the bottles. The husband watching his partner disappear into something. They get told to find a meeting or read a book — support groups for families have existed since 1951, and in all that time nobody built them software.

Which one should you pick?

Quitting or cutting back on alcohol only: Reframe or Sunnyside.
Just want a free counter: Nomo.
Want the biggest community: I Am Sober or Sober Time.
Rebuilding a social life sober: Loosid.
More than one habit, or a partner who's drowning in this too: that's exactly what ours is for.

Whichever you choose — choosing one tonight beats researching all of them for another week. Someday is the enemy.
