Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Let's start with something unusual for a comparison page: if gambling is the problem, you should probably install BetBlocker regardless of what else you choose. It's free, it's run by a charity, and it costs you nothing to have it standing guard. The real question is what you use alongside it.

What BetBlocker does well

BetBlocker

Free gambling blocking, run by a registered charity

BetBlocker blocks access to hundreds of thousands of gambling sites and apps across your devices. You pick an exclusion period — from a day up to years — and once it's set, it stays set until the time runs out. No account needed, fully anonymous, no subscription, no upsell, ever. As a pure wall between you and the bookmakers, it's the best free tool in existence, and the fact that a nonprofit built it tells you why.

Completely free — funded by donations

Where it may not fit

BetBlocker is a wall, not a companion. It doesn't count your days, teach you anything about why the urge comes, or help you through the hour when the urge is screaming and your phone is right there. It has no journal, no program, no one to talk to.

And a wall says nothing to the person who found the bank statements. The spouse or partner who's rebuilding trust — or deciding whether they can — needs support of their own, and blocking software was never meant to be that.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour. Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.
