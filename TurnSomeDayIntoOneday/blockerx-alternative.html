Looking for a BlockerX alternative?

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Anyone who's fought a screen habit knows the two-front truth: you need friction between you and the content, and you need something changing on the inside so the friction eventually isn't necessary. BlockerX is mostly the first front. Here's the honest lay of the land.

What BlockerX does well

BlockerX

A flexible blocker with accountability built in

BlockerX blocks adult sites from a large database and lets you go further: custom websites, keywords, even other apps — social media, gaming, gambling — if those are your leaks. The accountability-buddy option means someone you choose gets notified about changes, which makes uninstalling in a weak moment a lot less quiet. It runs on phones, desktop, and as a browser extension, there's a free basic tier, and it has an active community forum plus some courses and articles on the recovery side.

Free basics · premium subscription or lifetime — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

BlockerX leads with the wall; the inner-work side — the courses and content — is the supporting act, not the main event. If you've been through a blocker phase before, you already know the pattern: the block holds until the night you get creative. What changes that isn't a longer blocklist; it's the daily work on why you reach for it at all.

The full feature set also sits behind a premium tier that costs real money annually. And there's nothing in BlockerX for a partner who knows — their hurt doesn't have a tab.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour. Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.

Which should you pick?

If what you're missing is friction — a real barrier plus a buddy who gets pinged if you weaken it — BlockerX does that job well, and Day One doesn't block anything. Different tools.

If what you're missing is the change underneath — a program you actually follow, help in the urgent moments, and a place for the person who loves you — that's ours. Honestly, blocker plus daily work is a stronger setup than either alone, and our core app is free, so adding it costs nothing but honesty.
