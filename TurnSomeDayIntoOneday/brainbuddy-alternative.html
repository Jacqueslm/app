Looking for a Brainbuddy alternative?

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Brainbuddy was one of the first apps to treat quitting porn as something you work at daily rather than something you white-knuckle. It's been around a long time, and that history shows — in good ways and in ways that might send you looking for an alternative.

What Brainbuddy does well

Brainbuddy

One of the longest-running porn recovery programs

The heart of Brainbuddy is a 100-day program: daily tasks, breathing and mindfulness exercises, journaling, and a progress system that makes each day feel like it counts — many users mention the growing "Life Tree" as the thing that kept them coming back. There's a supportive community inside, and the whole experience is built around momentum: do today's piece, come back tomorrow.

Paid subscription after a short trial — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

Brainbuddy is porn-only and subscription-only — after the trial, essentially everything sits behind the paywall. If you want to try the daily-program way of working without paying first, or your budget is already stretched, that's a real consideration.

It's also built for one person on the journey. If a partner knows and is hurting — and with this habit, the partner's hurt is its own storm — there's no part of Brainbuddy for them.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour. Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.

Which should you pick?

If the gamified 100-day structure is what hooks you — the tree, the tasks, the daily streak — and the monthly cost is fine, Brainbuddy has helped a lot of people and could help you too.

Pick Day One if you want to start free today rather than after a checkout page, if porn is one of several habits you're untangling, or if part of what you're repairing is a relationship. Our lessons run in 30-day programs you can listen to like a podcast, and the partner side means the person who loves you isn't left outside the process. Either way — start one of them tonight, not someday.
