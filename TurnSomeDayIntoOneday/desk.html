Structure, off the candles
No candles loaded, so there is nothing to read yet.
A swing is a bar higher (or lower) than the two bars either side of it. HH higher high, HL higher low, LH lower high, LL lower low — each against the last swing of the same kind. Compressed means the last five bars span under three average bars. This is what has already happened; it says nothing about what comes next.
Study the timing
Nothing loaded yet. This pulls 500 bars on each timeframe from the same feed — about three months of 4h and five days of 15m — and counts what your own rules did in them.
Four counts and nothing else: how often a 4h level was only poked and never closed above, which is the grab; how far the down legs in these bars came back to the high that started them; how many 15m bars a higher low lasted before the first close below it; and how much of a day's range was made inside your session window on your own clock. Every number carries the count it came from. Nothing here is a backtest and nothing here is a forecast.
