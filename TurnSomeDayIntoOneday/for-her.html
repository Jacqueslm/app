The part nobody else built.

Every other app in this space treats the partner like a bystander. She isn't. She's the one who found it. She's the one who's been told she's overreacting. She's the one doing the math on whether to stay.

Inside, she gets her own space:

  • What's actually going on, explained without excusing it
  • What's hers to carry and what genuinely isn't
  • How to tell the difference between a promise and a change
  • What to do on the days she doesn't believe him
  • Language for the conversation neither of you knows how to start

She doesn't need his permission and she doesn't need his password.

Five short emails, one a day

If tonight is worse than that

If you're in crisis, or you've had thoughts of hurting yourself, please talk to a person tonight — not an app. Call or text 988 (US), any hour, free and confidential.

If you're afraid of him — not just hurt, afraid — the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. You don't have to be in danger tonight to call. You're allowed to call just to ask whether what you're living with is normal.
