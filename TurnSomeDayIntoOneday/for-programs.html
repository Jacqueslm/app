Straight answers

Is this HIPAA compliant?

No protected health information ever reaches us. The app works with no account and no name, and journals stay on the person's device. There is nothing to sign because there is nothing to share.

What's the catch on "free"?

The recovery tools and the first half of the program are free forever, funded by an optional $9.99 subscription for the second half and the AI companion. Your clients are never pushed toward it, and there are no ads anywhere.

We already recommend an app.

Good. Which one — and does it have anything for the family? That's the piece I'd add rather than replace.

What do we actually have to do?

Nothing. No seats to provision, no logins, no IT review. You put a card or a QR code in the discharge folder and hand one to the family.

Who built it?

Jacques Malone. I was addicted for thirty-eight years and got free at fifty. I built this because everything I could find cost money I didn't have or was written by someone who had never done it.
