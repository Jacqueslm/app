Looking for a Fortify alternative?

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Fortify has been in the porn-recovery space longer than almost anyone, and it takes the work seriously — courses, community, real structure. If you're weighing it against alternatives, here's the honest picture from someone who's been on the user side of this fight.

What Fortify does well

Fortify

A course-based recovery platform with a free way in

Fortify is built like a school for getting free: video lessons, training modules, journaling, progress tracking, and a community of people doing the same work. Two things stand out. First, there's a genuinely free basic tier — rare in this niche. Second, you can invite an ally to walk alongside you, which turns a private struggle into a shared project. Premium unlocks the full library and live sessions with people who know this terrain.

Free basic tier · premium subscription — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

Fortify is focused on one habit. If your struggle spreads wider — the porn and the drinking feed each other, or the same escape-hatch instinct shows up in three different places — you'd be working one front of a wider battle.

It's also course-first. If you learn best that way, great; if sitting through video modules feels like homework you'll quietly stop doing, be honest with yourself about that. And while the ally feature is real, there's no dedicated, private space built for a partner's own experience — their fear, their anger, their questions. Allies support your journey; nobody supports theirs.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour. Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.

Which should you pick?

If you want a deep, single-focus course platform and you like learning through video, Fortify is a solid choice — and since both apps have free tiers, you can honestly try both this week and see which one you still open on day ten. That test beats any comparison page, including this one.

Lean toward Day One if your habits come as a tangle rather than one thread, if you want short daily lessons you can listen to rather than watch, or if the person who loves you needs more than an invitation to observe your progress — they need a place of their own.
