Withdrawal timeline
Personalized phases with tools and warnings.
Education + support
This timeline is a general guide. Your experience may differ. If anything feels severe or unsafe, medical support is always the right move.
Hours 6–24 · Early withdrawal
Your body is healing in real time.
Anxiety, shaking, sweating, nausea, and headaches can begin. Heart rate and blood pressure may rise. This is your body adjusting to life without the substance.
⚠️ If you experience severe symptoms (confusion, chest pain, seizures), seek medical support immediately.
Days 1–3 · Peak withdrawal
The hardest window — but it ends.
Symptoms often peak here. Sleep is disrupted, irritability is intense, and cravings can feel overwhelming. This is where support and structure matter most.
⚠️ Medically supervised detox is strongly recommended during this phase for many substances.
Days 4–7 · Acute phase easing
Physical symptoms begin to ease.
Physical symptoms start to calm down. Mood swings and cravings may continue, but you’ll have more clear‑headed moments. This is a powerful time to build routines.
