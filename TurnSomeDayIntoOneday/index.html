Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Turn Someday Into Day One ("the app") is a recovery companion. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, where it lives, and who else ever sees it.

What we collect

When you create an account: your email address, a phone number if you choose to add one, and a password or PIN (stored as an irreversible hash — never in plain text, and never visible to us).

As you use the app: your recovery start date, journal entries, craving logs, mood check-ins, lesson progress, milestone history, and messages you send to Nova (the AI companion). All of this exists so the app can function — to show your streak, resume your lessons, and let Nova respond with real context.

Where it lives

This app is self-hosted: your data is stored in a local database on the server you (or whoever runs this instance) is running — not in a shared cloud service we control. If you're using an instance someone else operates, they are the ones storing your data, on their own server.

Who else sees it

Two outside services are used, and only for the specific job each does:

• Anthropic processes the messages you send to Nova, in order to generate Nova's replies. This only happens for the message itself — sent when you chat, nothing else is shared. Nova is only available when the app operator has configured this connection.

• Stripe processes payment if you subscribe to a paid plan. Your card details go directly to Stripe — this app never sees or stores your full card number.

Your journal, craving logs, and personal notes are never sent to either of these services unless you paste them into a message to Nova yourself.

We do not sell data. There is no advertising network or analytics tracker collecting your activity in this app.

Your controls

From Profile, at any time: Export my data downloads everything as a file. Delete my account or Erase everything & start over permanently removes your account and all associated data from the server — this cannot be undone once confirmed.

Not a substitute for professional care

Nova is a supportive tool, not a licensed therapist, doctor, or crisis service. In an emergency, or if you're in crisis, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or your local emergency number — not this app.

Who this app is for

This app discusses addiction, recovery, and related sensitive topics and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you've seen and accepted the update.
