I've tried apps before and quit in a week.
So did I, repeatedly. The difference here isn't motivation — it's that the plan gets written before you need it, and there's something to talk to at the hour you actually need it.

No. It's not treatment and it's not medical advice. It's a structured tool built by someone who lived it. If you need clinical help, get clinical help — this works alongside that, not instead of it.
