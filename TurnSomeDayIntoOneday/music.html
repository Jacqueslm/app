    Cut one out

    Ringtone maker

    Choose a song, drag the two handles to the part you want, listen to it, then save the clip. A ringtone sounds best at 15–30 seconds.

    Saves a WAV file of the clip. On an Android phone you can set it as your ringtone from the file. On an iPhone it needs to be an .m4r in the Ringtone folder, so keep it as a sound for now.
