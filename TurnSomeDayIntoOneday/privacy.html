Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Turn Someday Into Day One ("the app") is a recovery companion. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, where it lives, and who else ever sees it.

What we collect

When you create an account: your email address, a phone number if you choose to add one, and a password or PIN (stored as an irreversible hash — never in plain text, and never visible to us).

As you use the app: your recovery start date, journal entries, craving logs, mood check-ins, lesson progress, and milestone history. All of this exists so the app can function — to show your streak and resume your lessons.

Things you choose to write for other people are stored differently, because they have to leave your own account to work:

• Live room posts. If you post in a room, the text is stored on the server and shown to other members under the display name you choose. Every post is checked by an automated moderator before anyone sees it.

• Letters. A letter you write to a partner is stored on the server behind a private link so the person you send it to can open it. You can revoke a letter at any time, and letters expire on their own.

• Reviews. If you leave a review, the text, your first name and your star rating are stored and may be shown publicly on the reviews page.

Messages to Friendly are not stored. They are sent to the AI provider to generate a reply and are not written to the database or kept on the server; the app only counts how many chats you have used that day. They are cleared from the app when you close it.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in a database on the server that runs this app, operated by Turn Someday Into Day One and hosted in the United States. It is not sold, and it is not placed in any advertising or data-broker system.

The recorded lesson and story audio is served from a public file host. Playing it sends no account information — the request is for an audio file and nothing else.

Who else sees it

A small number of outside services are used, each for one specific job and nothing else:

• Google processes the messages you send to Friendly, in order to generate the replies, and also checks live room posts against the moderation rules before anyone sees them. Only the message, or the post, is sent.

• Resend delivers the emails this app sends you — your welcome, and your password reset link. It receives your email address and the message itself.

Your journal, craving logs, and personal notes are never sent to any of these services unless you paste them into a message to Friendly yourself.

We do not sell data. There is no advertising network, and nothing here follows you around other websites.

Your controls

From Profile, at any time: Export my data downloads everything as a file. Delete my account permanently removes your account and all associated data from the server — this cannot be undone once confirmed. Step-by-step instructions, and exactly what is deleted and what the payment providers keep, are on the delete your account page.

Not a substitute for professional care

Friendly is a supportive tool, not a licensed therapist, doctor, or crisis service. In an emergency, or if you're in crisis, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or your local emergency number — not this app.

Who this app is for

This app discusses addiction, recovery, and related sensitive topics and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you've seen and accepted the update.
