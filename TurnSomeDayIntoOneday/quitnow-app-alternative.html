Looking for a QuitNow alternative?

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

QuitNow has been helping people quit smoking for well over a decade, and if you're a smoker deciding between it and something newer, the honest answer depends less on features and more on what kind of quitter you are.

What QuitNow does well

QuitNow

The veteran quit-smoking tracker

QuitNow turns quitting into numbers you can watch grow: time since your last cigarette, cigarettes not smoked, money saved, and health milestones as your body recovers. For a lot of smokers, watching the money-saved figure climb past a car payment is worth more than any pep talk. There's a community chat full of ex-smokers who get it, and the Pro version adds achievements, a fuller experience, and structured guidance for the quitting process.

Free with ads · Pro upgrade — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

QuitNow is about cigarettes. That's its strength and its boundary. If smoking is your only habit, that focus works for you. But smoking rarely travels alone — it pairs with the evening drinks, the stress, the scrolling — and QuitNow isn't built to see any of that.

It's also stats-first. The numbers motivate you on good days; on the bad night when you're standing outside the gas station talking yourself into just one pack, a counter can't talk back. And there's nothing in it for the partner who's watched you quit four times and is scared to hope again.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour (that one's part of Pro). Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.

Which should you pick?

If cigarettes are your one battle and numbers fire you up, QuitNow is a fine, proven choice — keep it simple and let the money-saved counter do its work.

Pick Day One if the smoking is part of a bigger pattern you're finally ready to face whole, if you want a daily lesson and a companion for the cravings the counter can't answer, or if someone who loves you has been holding their breath through every previous attempt and deserves support of their own this time.
