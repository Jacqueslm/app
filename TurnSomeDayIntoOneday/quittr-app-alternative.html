The short answer

Quittr is slick, focused, and takes you seriously — it does one thing and commits. The question is whether porn is really one thread or one strand of a knot.

What Quittr does well

Quittr

A focused, modern program for quitting porn

Quittr does one thing and commits to it: a structured program for quitting porn, with streak tracking, a large content library, a community of people on the same road, and an in-the-moment tool for riding out an urge when it hits. The app is slick, the onboarding takes you seriously, and having everything aimed at a single goal keeps it from feeling scattered.

Subscription after a trial — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

Quittr is a single-track app. If porn is one thread in a bigger knot — the late nights also involve drinking, or the same restlessness shows up as gaming or gambling — you'd need a different app for each, and most of us don't keep four recovery apps alive at once.

It's also built around a paid subscription, so it's worth being sure it fits before you commit. And there's nothing in it for a spouse or partner who knows, or suspects, and is quietly carrying that alone.

One thread, or the whole knot

Single-track apps are cleaner. Everything points one way, nothing is diluted, and Quittr's in-the-moment urge tool is a genuinely good piece of design. If porn is the whole of it, that focus is an advantage, not a compromise.

For a lot of people it isn't the whole of it. The late nights involve drinking. The same restlessness turns up as gaming, or the scroll, or the spending. And nobody keeps four recovery apps alive at once — you pick one, the others quietly run, and eventually they pull the first one down.

Thirteen tracks here, running together if you need them, one daily lesson that knows about the others. Plus the 2am layer: breathing, grounding, a 2.5-minute exercise that rides the urge over its peak, six voices that talk you through it aloud, and Friendly to talk to when it's late.

And the money question. The first fifteen days of every track are free permanently, along with every tool, the journal and the partner side — so you can find out whether it fits before anything is charged.

Which should you pick?

Pick Quittr if porn is the single thing, you want a modern focused programme, and a subscription is fine. It's well made and it doesn't pretend to be more than it is.

Pick this if there's more than one thread, if you want to start without a card, or if a partner already knows and is carrying it by themselves.
