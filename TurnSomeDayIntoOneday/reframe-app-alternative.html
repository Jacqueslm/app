Looking for a Reframe alternative?

Updated August 2026

Full disclosure: this page lives on the site of one of the apps compared — ours. We'll still be straight with you about both, because different apps genuinely fit different people, and the fastest way to lose your trust would be to pretend otherwise.

Reframe is one of the most polished apps ever built for changing a drinking habit, and if you're comparing it to alternatives, chances are one of two things is true: the price gave you pause, or you realized your situation is bigger than alcohol alone. Both are worth an honest look.

What Reframe does well

Reframe

The most structured daily program for drinking less or quitting

Reframe is a real course, not just a counter. Every day there's a short reading and an exercise, and the curriculum builds over weeks — it treats changing your drinking like something you learn, which for many people is exactly the frame that finally works. It supports both cutting back and quitting, and the drink tracking and progress tools are genuinely well made.

Paid subscription, typically sold annually — check their site for current pricing

Where it may not fit

Reframe is about alcohol. Full stop. If your struggle is gambling, porn, smoking, food, or more than one thing at once — and for a lot of us it's never just one thing — it isn't built for that.

It's also a paid product from early on. That's a fair trade for the depth of the program, but if money's tight right now, it matters. And like nearly every app in this space, it's for the drinker alone: the husband or wife watching from across the kitchen table has no door to walk through.

How Day One is different

Turn Someday Into Day One

The one with a side for the person who loves them

Built by one person who was addicted for 38 years and got free at 50 — then built the app he'd needed the whole time. It covers 13 habit tracks: alcohol, porn, smoking, substances, gambling, social media, gaming, food, shopping, work, anger, supporting someone, and other.

There are SOS tools for the 2am moment — guided breathing, grounding, and recorded "talk me through it" voices. Friendly, an AI recovery companion you can talk to at any hour. Daily 30-day lesson programs with listen-along audio.

And the thing nobody else built: a whole private side for the partner — the person lying awake wondering what to do. Any gender, any relationship. Their side is private; what they write is theirs alone.

Which should you pick?

Honestly: if alcohol is your one and only battle, you want a deep daily curriculum, and the price doesn't sting, Reframe is a strong choice — arguably the strongest single-focus alcohol program on the market.

Pick Day One if you want a free way to start today, if your struggle touches more than one habit, or if someone who loves you is carrying this too and needs their own support — not a pamphlet, an actual place in the app. That last part exists nowhere else that we know of.
