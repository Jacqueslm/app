## Notes on wording

- No "recovery from addiction", no "treatment", no "therapy", no "cure" — all
  read as medical claims to a Play reviewer.
- "Habit support", "wellness companion", "daily companion" are the safe register
  and are what the health declaration will also say. **These two documents must
  agree.** If you change the register here, change it there too.
- The word "private" is doing real work: it is accurate, it is the strongest
  thing about the product, and it carries no policy risk.
