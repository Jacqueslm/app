How to read it

Frames a second — how often your body position is read. Under 15 and a dodge lands too late to count.

Lag — how long between moving and the app knowing. Over about 120ms you feel it.

Turn the fight on and watch both. That second reading is the real answer, because in the real thing the phone is drawing the ring at the same time.
