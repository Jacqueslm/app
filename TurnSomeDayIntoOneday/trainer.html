Safety instructions — read before training

The programme below is a workout, and two lines outrank it.

A doctor's rule wins over this page, always. If a doctor has said no to a lift or a movement, that is the answer here, and nothing on this page changes it.

If there is a heart condition, blood pressure, a pregnancy, a recent operation or a back that has gone recently, ask a doctor before starting anything — and if any of it is already being looked at or medicated, that stays exactly as it is.

And stop, on the day, if there is chest pain, or dizziness, or something sharp rather than a muscle working. That is not pushing on.

The P90X rotation

Workout instructions and schedule

This is P90X's classic ninety-day rotation: thirteen weeks in three phases, with a recovery week at the end of each phase, and six days on and one off every week.

This is how the by-10 sits on it. Pick a dumbbell they can pump out reps with, not the heaviest they can lift. How many sets they do depends on where they are starting, not on how advanced they are — two to five sets, and twenty reps at most in a set. Then ten seconds between exercises, and on to the next one. The order of the sessions is P90X's own.

Two sessions a day is how it is trained: one with the weights, one for the heart and lungs. The by-10 can be done every day, as long as the days rotate. P90X's cardio days can be swapped or mixed — jump rope, a step session, walking, dancing — and which one it is matters much less than that it happens.

A beginner starts with a week of stretching on its own, before any dumbbell, to get the body used to moving and keep it from getting hurt.

Phase 1 (weeks 1–3) and Phase 2 (weeks 5–7) each use one resistance block: Chest & Back, Plyometrics, Shoulders & Arms, Yoga X, Legs & Back, Kenpo X; then Chest, Shoulders & Triceps, Plyometrics, Back & Biceps, Yoga X, Legs & Back, Kenpo X. Phase 3 (weeks 9–12) alternates the two week by week — weeks 9 and 11 are the Chest & Back block, weeks 10 and 12 the Chest, Shoulders & Triceps block. The five resistance days finish with Ab Ripper X, sixteen minutes.

Weeks 4, 8 and 13 are recovery weeks, and they are the same shape but no load: Yoga X, Core Synergistics, Kenpo X, X Stretch, Core Synergistics, Yoga X, then rest. They are P90X's weeks and the by-10 does not require them: go straight into the next rotation, or take a full week of cardio and a little stretching instead, whichever the day asks for. The last one closes the ninety days.
