SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL EARLY-'90s WEST COAST POP-RAP

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box)
Early-1990s West Coast pop-rap in a warm, radio-friendly R&B groove, 102 BPM, G major. Swung funk drum-machine loop with a fat cracking snare, tight hi-hats, tambourine on the backbeat and a round, bouncing slap-style bass carrying the melody. Clean chicken-scratch electric guitar, short muted horn stabs and a warm electric-piano chord bed. Playful, conversational male lead, half-rapped and half-sung, close-mic'd and relaxed, with a light female "ooh" harmony answering the hook and a spoken intro over the loop. Call-and-response chant hook, handclaps on the turnaround, one brief drum fill and a stripped-down break before the final hook. Bright, punchy analog late-80s mix with the bass forward and the vocal sitting on top. Flirty, confident and clean throughout, dance-floor chemistry. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
