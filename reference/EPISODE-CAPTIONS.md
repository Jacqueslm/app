## The four rules these all follow

**1. TikTok and YouTube are search engines now.** The first words carry the
search phrase. `how to stop gambling` is 5,400 a month; `shopping addiction
help` is 880 at difficulty 41 — the cheapest of the four episodes.

**2. Instagram hides everything after the first line.** So the first line is the
whole caption and everything after it is for people who already tapped more.

**3. Facebook doesn't do hashtags.** Older audience, plain sentences travel
further, and a wall of tags reads as spam there. Your Facebook numbers are your
best numbers — 3,780 — so this one is written longest.

**4. Never explain that it's two men or two women.** It just is. The story
doesn't announce it and neither do these. What is *said* about the app never
assumes a shape of couple — that rule holds everywhere.

## Two notes before you post

**Don't post both episodes the same day.** Same series, same look — back to back
they compete with each other and the second one gets half the room. Two or three
days apart.

**Watch average view duration, not views.** Ten seconds held on a 15-second
piece beats a thousand people who left at three. That number tells you which
opening line to keep; view count only tells you what the algorithm did that day.
