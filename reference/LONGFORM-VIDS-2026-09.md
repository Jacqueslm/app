# Five long-form videos, from the keyword research

Titles are written the way people search now — a whole question, not a label.
Four of the five are **unrestricted topics**, so unlike the quit-porn video
they can actually get recommended.

---

## 1. "Why do I pick up my phone when I don't even want to?"
**~8 min · unrestricted · the biggest keyword you have**

`screen time` is the most frequent phrase in all 2,500 scraped reviews. Nobody
searching it calls themselves an addict, which is why it's the widest door.

- The hand moves before the decision does. Name it.
- The four hours nobody counts — evening, alone, nothing on.
- Why no rock bottom is coming for this one, and what that means
- The same loop as everything else people quit, without the ugly name
- What one day of counting actually changes

---

## 2. "I read 2,500 reviews of sobriety apps. Here's what people actually say."
**~12 min · unrestricted · nobody else can make this video**

You already scraped it. This is the one piece of content no competitor can
copy, because it's your data.

- What I did and why (2,500 reviews, 25 apps)
- The word that comes up most: *screen time*
- 165 reviews about paywalls — and two thirds are one-star
- The pattern: people don't quit the app, they quit at the card
- What I built instead, and what's actually free
- What I got wrong (say at least one thing)

---

## 3. "How do you help someone who doesn't want help?"
**~10 min · unrestricted · you are living this one**

No page, no video, big search. You are on the supporter side right now.

- The honest answer up front: you can't make anybody stop
- What you can do — the difference between a boundary and a rule for them
- Why checking, counting and searching never once changed anything
- The bit nobody says: you are allowed to be furious and still love them
- Your own life is not the thing to pause until they're better

---

## 4. "What actually happens in the first 30 days without drinking"
**~12 min · restricted-adjacent, but search-driven**

Your withdrawal-timeline page is the strongest AI-answer page you have. This is
the video of it.

- Days 1–3, 4–7, week two, week three, day thirty — plainly, no medical claims
- Why week two is where most people stop, and it isn't willpower
- The good day that arrives early and frightens people
- What is still hard at thirty days, said honestly
- What comes after

---

## 5. "Nobody warns you about the boredom"
**~7 min · unrestricted · the most distinctive one here**

The empty hours. It's in the app already, on floors 31–40, and almost nobody
in this category talks about it.

- You get four hours a night back and they are not a gift
- Boredom treated as a joke in recovery, and why it's one of the big ones
- The habit was doing a job. The job is still open.
- Filling it badly first — that's the normal order
- Five months before an empty evening stops feeling like a threat

---

**Order to film:** 2, then 1, then 3. Number 2 is the one that earns a
subscriber, because it's evidence nobody else has. Numbers 1 and 3 are the
biggest searches. Leave 4 for once the channel has some history — restricted
topics need an established channel to travel at all.
