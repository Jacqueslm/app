# Cards — 16 shorts, 7 to 15 seconds each

Images: `reference/partner-cards/`. Regenerate or reword any of them by editing
`CARDS` in `reference/make-partner-cards.py` and re-running it.

**Changed 11 Aug 2026:** shorts only, nothing long-form, and the app is now
advertised as **free with a paid upgrade**. Both of those changed the cards.

---

## What changed and why

**Every card is now two frames, not one.**

    01-not-the-only-one-tired-a.png    the big line on its own
    01-not-the-only-one-tired-b.png    the big line with the quiet line under it

A single still card held for 13 seconds is a poster somebody scrolls past. The
same words with a **cut in the middle** is a thought landing — you read the
line, there's a beat, then the second line arrives and turns it. That beat is
the entire reason these travel, and a one-frame card throws it away.

The big line sits in exactly the same place on both frames, so the cut adds a
line instead of jumping the layout.

**"FREE" is now on every card**, in the one green thing on screen, above the
brand line at the foot. Nothing on the old cards ever said the app was free.
Free is the word that makes a stranger tap.

**There's a closing frame**: `99-free-end.png` (and a 2x). FREE TO USE / *No
card. No trial. Upgrade only if you want more.* Every short ends on it.

**Four new cards, 13 to 16**, that say it out loud rather than only at the foot.

## How to build one — 13 seconds

In Studio, three shots, **Still** on all three:

| Shot | File | Seconds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `NN-name-a.png` | 4 |
| 2 | `NN-name-b.png` | 6 |
| 3 | `99-free-end.png` | 3 |

**Cuts, not blends.** A crossfade between two frames that share a layout looks
like a rendering fault. Still matters too — any camera move resamples flat type
and softens it.

That's 13 seconds. If you want it tighter, 3 / 5 / 3 gives you 11. Don't go
under 7 or over 15.

**Or straight in the app:** post the `-b` frame as a photo and pick a sound in
TikTok. Faster, and in-app sounds get treated better than uploaded audio — but
you lose the beat, which is most of what makes it work. Use this when you're
short on time, not by default.

## Hashtags

Three or four, not thirty. Same set every time so the account gets classified
consistently:

```
#betrayaltrauma #lovinganaddict #recoverysupport #alanon
```

Swap `#alanon` for `#soberpartner` on anything about drinking specifically.
On cards 13 to 16, swap one for `#freeapp` — that's the one people search.

---

## One a day, not sixteen at once

Sixteen posts in an afternoon trains the feed to treat the account as a dump.
One a day for sixteen days is the same work and a completely different signal.

---

## What to watch after a week

Not likes — **followers and saves**. These are built to be saved and sent to
someone, which is the behaviour that moves a small account. If views climb and
follows don't, the cards are landing and the bio isn't; that's a bio problem,
not a card problem.

**The number that decides whether the two-frame cut was worth it:** average
watch time. YouTube was sitting at about seven seconds a view across 2,271
views. A 13-second short that gets nine seconds is working. One that still gets
seven means the second frame isn't earning its place, and you'd go to 3/5/3.
