# Partner cards — 12 posts

Images: `reference/partner-cards/`. Regenerate or reword any of them by editing
`CARDS` in `reference/make-partner-cards.py` and re-running it.

## Why these and not more talking heads

On 9 Aug the TikTok account read: 4 followers, 89 likes, and per-video views
between 26 and 128. Six of the top-row thumbnails were the same shot — face,
white wall, looking at camera. The ones that travelled were the plain text
cards, and every one of those was written to the **partner**, not the person
using.

That is also the thing no competitor has, the thing the rehab and school emails
lead with, and the half of this audience nobody else writes for. So the batch is
all partner, no exceptions.

**The format that's working, and every card below keeps it:** one line somebody
recognises themselves in, then one quiet line underneath that turns the knife or
lets them off the hook. Nothing else on screen.

---

## How to post them

**In Studio:** one card, **Still**, **6–7 seconds**, one quiet track under it.
Still matters — any camera move resamples flat type and softens it.

**Or straight in the app:** post as a photo and pick a sound in TikTok. Faster,
and in-app sounds get treated better than uploaded audio.

**One a day, not twelve at once.** Twelve posts in an afternoon trains the feed
to treat the account as a dump. One a day for twelve days is the same work and a
completely different signal.

## What to watch after a week

Not likes — **followers and saves**. These cards are built to be saved and sent
to someone, which is the behaviour that moves a small account. If views climb
and follows don't, the cards are landing and the bio isn't; that's a bio
problem, not a card problem.
