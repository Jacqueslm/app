# The Couples series — one door per addiction

Started 12 Aug 2026. Every piece is the same shape: **a couple, one of them
using, and the story told from the side of the one who found out.**

## Why this is the door-opener

Every recovery app in `KEYWORDS.md` assumes the same couple — a man with a
problem and a wife who found out. So every other version of that sentence is
unclaimed, which is why the cheapest terms in the entire keyword file sit in
this lane.

Each episode opens a door nobody else is standing in:

| # | Couple | Addiction | Door it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two men | Gambling | Same-sex male couples · the only addiction with no physical tell |
| 2 | Two women | Shopping | Same-sex female couples · **880/mo, KD 41 — a Build term, not too hard** |
| 3 | *open* | Food / binge | 9,900/mo, the biggest winnable term you own, fully ad-safe |
| 4 | *open* | Doomscrolling | 1,600/mo, KD 24, youngest audience, cheapest reach |

**The rule that makes the series work:** the person using is never the villain
and never forgiven. Every episode has one line that says *they're not a bad
person* and one that says *that's what makes it hard*. That pair is what every
partner recognises and what no advert ever says.

## House look — paste at the end of EVERY image prompt

> cinematic photograph, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, warm practical
> light from lamps and screens only, muted colour grade, documentary realism,
> vertical 9:16 composition, ordinary American apartment, no text in the image

Keep each couple's descriptions **identical in every prompt** or the faces
drift between shots.

# EPISODE 1 · The Gambler — two men

> **DANNY:** Black man, late 30s, short locs, lean, glasses.
> **MARCUS:** white man, early 40s, close-cropped greying hair, heavier build, beard.

**Gambling has no tell.** No smell, no slurring, nothing in the bin. Every other
addiction announces itself on the body; this one only ever shows up in **the
money** — which is why the discovery happens in a banking app, and why the
partner is always last to know. Build the whole piece on that.

**SHOT 1** — `DANNY and MARCUS on a sofa in a lamplit living room at night, sitting close, both looking at their own phones, comfortable, nothing wrong`
*"Nothing looked wrong."*

**SHOT 2** — `Close on DANNY's hands holding a phone showing a banking app, the screen the only light on his face, his expression going still`
*"I was only checking the balance."*

**SHOT 3** — `Extreme close on a phone screen showing a long list of identical repeated transactions, slightly blurred, the repetition obvious, a thumb frozen mid-scroll`
*"The same charge. Over and over."*

**SHOT 4** — `DANNY looking up from the phone across the room at MARCUS, who is laughing at something on TV and hasn't noticed, the space between them wide`
*"He was laughing at something on TV."*

**SHOT 5** — `DANNY alone at a kitchen table late at night, laptop open, bank statements spread out, a pen in his hand, adding it up`
*"So I added it up. All of it."*

**SHOT 6** — `Close on a printed savings account statement on a kitchen table under a lamp, the balance column low, a coffee ring on the corner`
*"Four years of saving."*

**SHOT 7** — `A framed photograph on a shelf of DANNY and MARCUS together somewhere happy, slightly out of focus, foreground dark`
*"He's not a bad man."*

**SHOT 8** — `MARCUS asleep in bed in the dark, DANNY sitting up beside him awake, looking straight ahead at nothing`
*"That's what makes it hard."*

**SHOT 9** — `DANNY on the sofa at first light, grey morning through the window, phone in his hand, searching for something, face tired`
*"I didn't know who to ask."*

**SHOT 10** — `Wide of the same living room in full morning light, both men sitting on the sofa turned toward each other, mid-conversation, nothing resolved but the talking started`

# EPISODE 2 · The Packages — two women

> **ROSA:** Latina woman, mid 30s, long dark hair usually tied back, small build.
> **KAT:** white woman, late 30s, blonde bob, tall, freckles.

**Shopping's tell is the hiding, not the buying.** Nobody hides a jumper. They
hide *how many*. The tags left on, the bag brought in before you get home, "I've
had this ages" — the lie is small enough that you let it go the first fifty
times. That's what makes this one land: the partner isn't discovering a purchase,
she's discovering how long she's been managed.

Also the cheapest of the four for search — **shopping addiction help, 880/mo,
difficulty 41, tagged Build**, not too hard.

## How to build either one

**Cuts, not blends.** The rhythm is the point — it's the feeling of scrolling a
statement. A blend softens it.

**Still on the paper and the sleeping shots.** Flat paper and a face in the dark
both go mushy under a camera move.

Both run about **17 seconds**. Slightly over the window and worth it — the last
two shots need room. Trim shot 1 to 1.0s if you want under 15.

**Longer cut for YouTube and Facebook:** same order, 4 seconds a shot, 40
seconds, blends instead of cuts, music underneath. Don't post that one to TikTok.

**Tick "Made with AI"** on every post — Studio appends it automatically on the
AI presets.

## The two lines never to rewrite

Each episode has a pair:

> *"He's not a bad man." / "That's what makes it hard."*
> *"She's not greedy. She's not selfish." / "She's just scared of something she won't say."*

Nothing in these pieces blames the person using, and nothing forgives them
either. That pair is the entire emotional argument, it's what every partner
recognises, and no advert has ever said it. Everything else can be reworded.
