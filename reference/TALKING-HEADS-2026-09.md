# Two talking heads — built from the keyword research

Written 1 Sep 2026, straight out of `TurnSomeDayIntoOneday/store-listing/KEYWORD-RESEARCH.pdf`.
Same four beats as every ad in `ADS.md`: **Recognition → The cost → Free, no card → One action.**

Both are **Lane A** — neither mentions alcohol, drugs or porn, so neither needs
LegitScript certification. They can be filmed and posted this week.

Shot notes for both: you, straight to lens, one unbroken take, no intro, no
music under the voice. The frame never changes — that is the format working,
not a limitation. Shoot vertical.

---

## TH1 · "You didn't decide to pick it up"

**The keyword behind it.** `screen time` is the single most frequent phrase in
all 2,500 scraped reviews (50 mentions), and **six of the 25 apps** in the
scrape are screen-time apps. It is tracked inside the app and has **no page**.
It is also the one people search for without ever using the word *addiction* —
which is exactly why it is the widest door.

**Where:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts. **Format:** 15s vertical.

| Time | On screen | Voice | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | YOU DIDN'T DECIDE TO PICK IT UP | "Nobody decides to pick up their phone. Your hand just goes." | You, straight to lens |
| 3–8s | — | "It's the same loop as everything else people quit. It just doesn't have an ugly enough name yet." | Same frame, hold |
| 8–12s | — | "So nothing's coming to force it. No rock bottom. You just pick a day." | Same frame |
| 12–15s | **FREE · NO CARD** | *silent* | End card |

**Headline:** You didn't decide to pick it up
**Primary text:** Screen time is the one nobody calls an addiction, so nothing ever forces the issue. A free app that counts the days for you. No card, no trial.
**Tags:** #screentime #dopaminedetox #day1 #phoneaddiction

> **Why the third beat is the one that matters.** "No rock bottom is coming" is
> the true thing nobody says about this category. Drinking has a bottom that
> arrives on its own. Scrolling never does — you can do it every night for
> twenty years and nothing external ever stops you. That line is the whole ad.

## TH2 · "Every app that says free isn't"

**The keyword behind it.** 165 of the 2,500 reviews mention price, paywalls,
subscriptions or refunds — and **66% of those are one- or two-star**. "pay
wall", "free version", "cancel subscription" and "free trial" all rank in the
complaint list. This is not a discount message. It is the loudest unmet demand
in the whole category, and the only structural advantage a solo founder has.

**Where:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts. **Format:** 20s vertical.

| Time | On screen | Voice | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | EVERY APP THAT SAYS FREE ISN'T | "Every app that says free isn't." | You, straight to lens |
| 3–9s | — | "You find out on day eight. You go to open your own day count and it wants sixty dollars a year for it." | Same frame, hold |
| 9–15s | — | "Your streak. The thing you did. Behind a card." | Slight lean in, same frame |
| 15–18s | — | "Mine's free. The counter, the panic button, the journal. No card." | Same frame |
| 18–20s | **FREE · NO CARD** | *silent* | End card |

**Headline:** Every app that says free isn't
**Primary text:** Day eight, and your own day count is behind a paywall. The counter, the SOS button and the journal are free here. No card, no trial.
**Tags:** #sobertok #day1 #recoveryjourney #freeapp

> **Accuracy check before you film this.** Every claim in it is true of the
> app as shipped: the day counter, SOS set, breathing, "Talk me through it",
> the journal, weekly reports and the first 15 days of every pack are free
> with no card. Pro is days 16–90, Friendly and the rooms. Do not let the
> script drift into "everything is free" in a re-record.
>
> **Do not name a competitor on camera.** The sixty-dollar figure is real —
> it is the annual price of one of the apps in `ADS.md` — but naming anyone
> invites a takedown and adds nothing. "Sixty dollars a year" lands the same.
