**Description**

## Applies to all five

- **Thumbnail:** the biggest number or the shortest phrase, white on the dark
  navy. "2,500 REVIEWS". "NO ROCK BOTTOM". "YOU CAN'T." Don't put a sentence
  on a thumbnail.
- **First line of the description is the hook.** Everything after "…more" is
  for search.
- **Pinned comment:** ask the question the video asks. On 02: "What's the
  worst paywall you've hit?" Comments are the cheapest ranking signal there is.
- Set the category to **People & Blogs**, not Education. Education gets less
  suggested traffic.
