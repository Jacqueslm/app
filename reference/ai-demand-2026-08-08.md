# AI DEMAND — captured 8 Aug 2026, before the Semrush trial ended

From Semrush **AI Visibility → Prompt Research**, US, all AI platforms, data as
of 7 Aug 2026. Recorded because the trial is being cancelled and this tool is
paywalled.

**AI Volume is not Google search volume.** It counts how often people ask *AI
assistants* about a topic. Do not compare these numbers with `KEYWORDS.md` —
different metric, different scale. `KEYWORDS.md` is still the source of truth
for anything to do with ranking in Google.

Relevance is Semrush's own scoring of how closely the topic matches the query.
Only High and Medium rows are kept here.

---

## The pattern worth knowing

Across all three lanes, the biggest topics are **named methods and books**, not
generic questions:

| Topic | AI Volume | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Rethinking Drinking | 7.3K | the US government's NIAAA programme |
| Easy Way to Quit Drinking | 1.5K | Allen Carr's book |
| Kick the Drink | 1.3K | Jason Vale's book |
| Betrayal Bond | 3.1K | Patrick Carnes' book |
| Brain Over Binge | 258 | Kathryn Hansen's book |

When someone asks an AI how to stop, they ask for a **thing with a name** they
half-remember. The app has a name; the method inside it does not. The relapse
plan written at 2pm for the 2am version of you is the most distinctive thing in
the product and it is unnamed. That is a positioning question, not a task —
noted here so it doesn't get lost.

## What it means

**Betrayal trauma is the winnable lane, and by a distance.** On drinking you
would have to displace SAMHSA and Mayo Clinic; that is not happening. On
betrayal trauma the field is Reddit, YouTube, a magazine, a **dictionary** and
**Netflix** — against 70,200 in AI demand. There is no authoritative answer,
and the four partner pages are written by someone who lived the other side of
it. Same moat as always, confirmed from a new direction.

**Reddit is first or second in every lane.** AI leans on it harder than any
institution. Noted as fact, not as a recommendation: the standing rule is
Buffer only, never the apps, because those feeds are full of exactly what he is
recovering from — and recovery subreddits are relapse stories and drinking talk
all day. If Reddit is ever worth doing, the partner subreddits are a different
room from the drinking ones, and it is a deliberate decision, not something a
chart decides.

**Nobody is optimising for this yet.** No competitor app has any AI presence at
all. Whatever share exists here is unclaimed.
