## Do not disavow

Google ignores links a site didn't build. Disavow exists for manual penalties,
and there is no manual penalty here. Filing one would burn time and risk
telling Google to ignore something legitimate by mistake.

## What this actually means

**The referring-domain count is not a progress metric for this site.** It will
keep drifting up as more spam finds the domain, and it means nothing. Do not
read a rising number here as marketing working — I made exactly that mistake on
8 Aug before pulling the underlying list.

The real number to watch is **referring domains that are recovery, health or
media sites**. Today that is zero.

Which makes `OUTREACH.md` the only genuine link-building in play: 16 reachable
targets left, each one a real editorial mention on a treatment-centre or
health-media site. One of those outweighs all 14 domains above.
