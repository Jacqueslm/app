￾. Reproduction requirements and caveats
Use Python ￾ with Pillow only if you need to inspect or resize assets; the actual video
renderer requires FFmpeg with the following capabilities: libx￾￾￾ , the zoompan video filter,
the drawtext video filter, the concat filter, and a TrueType font at
/usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSans.ttf . On another machine, replace that font path
with an installed equivalent and update all absolute paths.
The source images are ￾￾￾￾×￾￾￾￾ PNGs, already in a ￾:￾￾ composition. The end-card
source is a ￾￾￾￾×￾￾￾￾ WEBP and is scaled to ￾￾￾￾×￾￾￾￾. The renderer does not crop the
story images; zoompan creates the still, zoom-in, and zoom-out motion from each native
vertical source.
To reproduce the exact final render, place the ten PNGs, the WEBP end card, and
render_eli_story.py at the paths shown above, ensure the caption files are generated with
the exact contents shown in Section ￾, and run:
Bash
python3 /home/ubuntu/render_eli_story.py
If reproducing outside /home/ubuntu , edit the absolute paths in the script. The generated
WAV music files are independent assets and are not required to reproduce the final video
because the final MP￾ contains no audio.
The final successful renderer logic, in compact form, was:
Python
# Per-shot processing:
ffmpeg -y -loop 1 -i <image> \\
 -vf "zoompan=z='<expression>':d=150:s=1080x1920:fps=30,drawtext=fontfile=/usr/
 -t 5.0 -an -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 18 <part-output>.mp4
# Story-only assembly:
ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i /home/ubuntu/eli_story_parts/concat.txt \\
 -an -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 18 \\
 /home/ubuntu/eli_story_parts/story_only.mp4
# End card:
ffmpeg -y -loop 1 -i /home/ubuntu/upload/99freeend2x.webp \\
 -vf "scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=1080:1920:(ow-iw
 -t 3 -an -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 18 \\
 /home/ubuntu/eli_story_parts/endcard.mp4
# Final assembly:
ffmpeg -y -i /home/ubuntu/eli_story_parts/story_only.mp4 \\
 -i /home/ubuntu/eli_story_parts/endcard.mp4 \\
 -filter_complex "[0:v][1:v]concat=n=2:v=1:a=0[v]" \\
 -map "[v]" -an -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 18 -movflags +faststart \\
 /home/ubuntu/eli_story_captioned.mp4
This recipe intentionally distinguishes between what was generated, what was actually
assembled into the MP￾, and what was not done. In particular, the music tracks were
generated but no audio mix or audio mux was performed in the final video.
