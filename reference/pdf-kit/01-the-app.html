What is inside

  • A sober day counter with a lifetime total that never resets — because a hard day should not erase a hard year.
  • A 90-day program in plain language, one short lesson a day, with separate tracks for drinking, vaping, gambling, porn, food, gaming, spending, work, anger and more.
  • Every lesson read aloud in a real voice, six narrators, so you can listen with the screen off or when reading is too much.
  • An SOS screen for the craving itself — guided breathing, grounding, and a hands-free "talk me through it" walkthrough for the worst ten minutes.
  • A private journal with mood tracking and prompts for when you do not know where to start.
  • Visible progress — streaks, hours won back, and milestone cards that show a number and nothing else, so what you are working on stays yours.
  • A climb up ninety carved steps, one for every day you show up. A slip dims the sky and never takes a step back.
  • Narrated stories from people who have lived it, from both sides — the person using and the person who loves them.
  • An app lock and a discreet mode — change the app's name and icon so nothing on your phone announces what this is.

The part nobody else builds

If you are not the one struggling but you love someone who is, this app is not an afterthought for you. You get your own track, in your own voice, about what is actually yours to carry — boundaries, the anger you learned to predict, and getting your own life back. It does not ask you to fix anyone.

Measured on the Play Store: of roughly 160 recovery apps, none serve the family this way.

What costs money, and what does not

The app is free to download and the core of it is free to keep using: the sober day counter, the first half of every program, all the SOS tools, reminders, insights, weekly reports, and the journal you can type or speak into. No card required to start and no time limit on the free version.

Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks the second half of every program, an AI companion to talk to at any hour, and the live community rooms. Monthly and yearly plans both begin with a 7-day free trial, and there is a one-time lifetime option. Cancel any time in two taps.

Privacy

Journals, notes, and anything said to the companion belong to the person who wrote them. There are no ads and no trackers, and nothing about anyone's recovery is sold or shared. The full policy is in plain language, in the app and on the website.
