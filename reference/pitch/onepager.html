Turn Someday Into Day One
Aftercare & family support · partner program

Your clients leave on a Friday. Then what?

The ninety days after discharge decide your outcomes, and they happen where you can't reach. This is a free app your clients keep in their pocket for those ninety days — and the first one built to also hand something to the family sitting in your waiting room.

What your clients get — free, forever

  • A 90-day program that starts the day they leave you
  • Tools for the worst ten minutes: breathing, grounding, a hands-free voice that talks them through it
  • A private journal nobody can read — no moderation, no deletion
  • A day counter that never shames a slip
  • Works with no account and no name. No card. No ads. Ever.

What your program gets

  • Continuity for 90 days past discharge, with your name on it
  • A real handout for the family program — the piece nobody else has
  • Nothing to administer, install, or support
  • No client data leaves the client's phone to us or to you
  • Printable cards and a QR code for the discharge folder
