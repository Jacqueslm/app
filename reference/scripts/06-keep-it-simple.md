# Keep it simple

**~3 min · unrestricted**

## COLD OPEN

The people I've watched make this stick did almost nothing.

That's the bit that annoys everybody. There's no system. No spreadsheet. They had one rule and they were boring about it for a very long time.

## THE PLAN IS NOT THE THING

When you decide to stop, the first thing your head wants to do is build something.

An app for tracking it. A morning routine. A reading list. A start date that lines up with a Monday. Maybe a whole new version of you, arriving next week, fully formed.

And it feels brilliant. It feels like progress. You go to bed that night genuinely pleased with yourself, and you have not yet done one single hard thing.

That's the trap. Planning is the most comfortable seat in the building. You can sit in it for years.

## COMPLICATED IS SOMEWHERE TO HIDE

Here's the uncomfortable version.

A complicated plan gives you a lot of places to fail that aren't the real one. You didn't do the journal. You missed the routine. The tracker's out of date. Now you're behind on your own system, you feel like a mess, and none of that was ever the actual job.

The actual job was one thing. And the more moving parts you build around it, the easier it is to be busy instead.

## WHAT SIMPLE ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE

One rule you can say in a breath.

"Not alone after ten." "I don't keep it in the house." "I ring somebody before, not after."

That's it. Not a philosophy. Not goals. A rule about what you do, short enough that you can remember it at eleven at night when you're tired and your judgement's gone — because that's the only moment it has to work in.

If you can't say it in one breath, it's too long to survive the night you need it.

## THE BORING PART IS THE WHOLE PART

Repetition is what does this. And repetition is dull, which is exactly why it works — dull is something you can do while tired, sad, bored or unimpressed with yourself.

Nobody's ever going to congratulate you for the two hundredth ordinary Tuesday. That's not a flaw in the plan. That IS the plan.

## CLOSE

One rule. Say it in a breath. Be boring about it.

If you're sat there tonight designing something, that's the thing to notice. Put the design down and do the small version now.
