SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL SOUL R&B "MY DAYS ARE NUMBERED"

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box — 952 characters)
Warm soul R&B ballad at 74 BPM in B-flat major over a slow 6/8 shuffle. Fender
Rhodes with gentle tremolo, upright bass walking the changes, live drums with
brushed snare and rim clicks, and a Hammond organ swell under each chorus. Strings enter at the second chorus and stay; a small choir answers the
lead in the final chorus and bridge. Close, breathy male soul tenor —
conversational in the verses, full and open on the chorus, natural runs and no
tuning sheen — double-tracked on hooks, harmony-stacked under the last chorus. Sparse first verse (Rhodes, bass, brushes), fuller second, stripped
bridge of organ and choir, the whole room on the out chorus before it falls
back to Rhodes and a spoken last line. Wide, warm analog mix: vocal forward,
bass round, cymbals soft. Celebratory and defiant rather than mournful — a life
celebrated, not mourned. Original lyrics, melody, cadence and arrangement; do
not recreate or imitate any existing song.

SHORT STYLE (use if the Style box rejects the long version — 306 characters)
Organ-and-choir soul R&B ballad, 74 BPM, Bb major, 6/8 shuffle, Rhodes, upright
bass, brushed drums, Hammond organ, strings from the second chorus, choir
answering the lead, close breathy male soul tenor with runs, stripped bridge
then full out-chorus, warm analog mix. Celebratory, defiant. Original song.
