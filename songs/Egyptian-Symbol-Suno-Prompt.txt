SUNO PROMPT — "EGYPTIAN SYMBOL"

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 810 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hip-hop dance-craze record in two gears: verses at 90 BPM pushing to 120 on the chorus, in E Phrygian dominant, lifting to F-sharp minor on the last chorus. Punchy boom-bap drums with a tight snare and tambourine, doubled by doumbek, frame drum and hand percussion, syncopated claps on the hook. A sinuous oud loop and a breathy ney flute trade with a fat synth bass and short horn stabs. Playful male lead, half-rapped and close, answered by a mixed group chant and a bright female ad-lib layer. Call-and-response counting lines, one breakdown of drums and claps only with the voice dropping out, then a double-time final chorus. Bright, dry, club-ready mix with round low end and percussion forward. Fun, confident, clean, built for a dance challenge. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
