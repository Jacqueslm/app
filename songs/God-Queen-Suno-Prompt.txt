SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL BLACK-WOMAN REBIRTH ANTHEM "GOD QUEEN"
The original God Queen, the abuse and erasure, the false savior and the system,
then the detox and the return home to herself.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 712 characters)

Inspirational female hip-hop with Black power energy at 91 BPM in C-sharp minor. Hardcore R&B trap drums, deep 808, trap hi-hats, warm Hammond organ, cinematic strings, boom-bap undertow and a bassline like a heartbeat. A Black woman leads with spoken-word verses and a sung, chanting chorus; a massed female choir answers behind her. The four-part chorus lands like a roll call; a low, distorted male voice repeats a wake-up warning underneath, half-buried in the mix. The beat starts still and sparse, then opens into a hard, triumphant trap-soul groove. Mood: grief turning to rage, then clarity and rebirth. No auto-tune, no pop polish, no minstrelsy. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
