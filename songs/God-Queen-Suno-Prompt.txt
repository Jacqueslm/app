SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL BLACK-WOMAN REBIRTH ANTHEM "GOD QUEEN"
The original God Queen, the abuse and erasure, the false savior and the system,
then the detox and the return home to herself. Seductive, synthesizer-free, all-female.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 904 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seductive inspirational female hip-hop at 91 BPM in C-sharp minor. Slow-burn R&B groove with live drums, deep upright bass, trap hi-hats, warm Hammond organ, muted trumpet, cinematic strings, finger snaps and a bassline like a heartbeat. One Black woman leads every verse, bridge and outro with close-mic, breathy, commanding spoken-word delivery; she also leads the sung, chanting chorus, with a massed female choir answering like a whisper behind her. The four-part chorus lands like a slow roll call; a low female choir hums a warning underneath, half-buried in the mix. The beat starts still and sparse, then opens into a hard, triumphant trap-soul groove. Mood: grief turning to rage, then clarity and rebirth. All vocals female: no male voice, no male ad-libs, no duet. No synthesizer of any kind, no auto-tune, no pop polish, no minstrelsy. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
