SUNO PROMPT - ORIGINAL HIP HOP ANTHEM "I CAN CHANGE, YOU CAN CHANGE"
One song in two tracks. Six verses of eight bars, eight choruses on five voices,
six rappers, plain human words only - nothing borrowed from any belief system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRACK ONE - STYLE OF MUSIC / AUDIO PROMPT (paste into the Style box - 753 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mid-tempo 92 BPM hip hop anthem in A minor that changes lanes three times: dusty boom-bap breakbeat, trap-soul 808s under the second verse, fat West Coast funk bass on the third. Deep kick, tight cracking snares, filtered vinyl texture, warm round sub bass, muted Rhodes chords, warm Hammond organ pad, low choir hum. Three different male rap voices, one lane each: clipped street reporter, slow Southern drawl, laid-back West Coast flow. Hook sung by five singers - alto lead, two tenors, soprano, baritone underneath - stacked three-part harmonies, handclap pattern, answered ad-libs between the lines. Cinematic, heavy, clear-eyed, never shouting. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern; do not recreate or imitate any existing song.
