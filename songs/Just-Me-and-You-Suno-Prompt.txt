Male lead in a low, intimate delivery; female counter-voice and whispered background taunts. Rain-on-the-window texture, late-night phone-line intimacy, a dusty sample chop under the Rhodes, and a low male harmony on the last chorus. Sensual, honest, cold when it needs to be, then warm again. No auto-tune, no EDM, no pop polish. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
