SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL CINEMATIC CONSCIOUS HIP-HOP "MASS OVERDOSE"
A slow-building awakening anthem about a world overdosing on poison and numbness.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 773 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cinematic conscious hip-hop at 80 BPM in F-sharp minor. Opens with a heartbeat, vinyl dust, lone piano and spoken word; builds through kick, snare, sub bass, strings, brass and a wide call-and-response choir. Male lead with a low, measured delivery that rises to a shout; female and crowd chants on the hook. Ad-libs all through: whispers, wake-up calls, count-offs. The beat is a slow, heavy head-nod, not trap. Strings swell, timpani enters, the final chorus is full band with double-time handclaps and a crowd chant. A low male hum under the first verse, a female answer on the pre-chorus, and a final a cappella chant before the last drop. Dark, urgent, uplifting, no comedy. No auto-tune, no EDM, no pop polish. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT STYLE (Style box if the long one is rejected - 299 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cinematic conscious hip-hop, 80 BPM, F-sharp minor. Heartbeat, piano, strings, brass, sub bass, wide choir. Male lead rises from measured to shout; female and crowd chants, ad-libs throughout. Slow head-nod build, full band final chorus. Dark, urgent, uplifting. No auto-tune, no EDM. Original song.
