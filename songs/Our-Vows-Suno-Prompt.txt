SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL WEDDING DUET "OUR VOWS"
A piano ballet duet for Jessica and Kourtney, written as two love letters:
Kourtney to Jessica, then Jessica to Kourtney, then the vows they make together.
Soulful male tenor and female alto, live piano and strings, no drums.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 734 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Piano-led ballet waltz at 68 BPM in E-flat major, 3/4 time. Solo grand piano carries the melody like a dance, with warm string quartet, harp, soft timpani and a final rise of strings. A soulful male tenor and female alto trade love-letter verses and join in close harmony on the chorus. The vocal is intimate and passionate, never shouted; the piano stays under every word. The arrangement moves from one piano and one voice to a full, tender duet, then returns to piano and whispered vows. A ballet pulse, not a pop beat; rubato at the turnarounds, a gentle lift into each chorus. Mood: elegant, romantic, grateful, forever. No drum kit, no synth, no auto-tune, no pop polish. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and piano figure.

Piano-led ballet waltz, 68 BPM, E-flat major, 3/4. Grand piano, strings, harp, soft timpani. Soulful male tenor and female alto trade verses, harmonize on the chorus. Intimate, passionate, elegant. No drums, no synth, no auto-tune. Original song.
