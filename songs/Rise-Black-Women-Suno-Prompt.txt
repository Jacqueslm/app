SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL ALL-FEMALE SISTERHOOD ANTHEM "RISE BLACK WOMEN"
Black women coming together in universal love, supporting each other, revealing
concealed history, and moving as a militant army of truth that holds hands and
hugs through the struggle. All female voices.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 764 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Militant all-female soul anthem at 98 BPM in F minor. Marching snare, foot stomps, handclaps, tambourine, deep upright bass, grand piano, brass section, cinematic strings and a massed female choir. One Black woman leads with a strong, warm alto; the verses are spoken-sung and close, while the chorus is a call-and-response chant with the choir answering every line. The arrangement starts with one voice and a handclap, then builds into a full sisterhood march, with a brass break and a final a cappella chant. The march is heavy but never rushed; the tenderness stays inside the fight. Mood: tender, defiant, protective, united. All female voices, no male lead. No auto-tune, no pop polish, no minstrelsy. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT STYLE (Style box if the long one is rejected - 264 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Militant all-female soul anthem, 98 BPM, F minor. Marching snare, stomps, claps, upright bass, piano, brass, strings, massed female choir. Spoken-sung verses, call-and-response chorus. Tender, defiant, united sisterhood. No auto-tune, no pop polish. Original song.

LYRICS (paste into the Lyrics box - 3751 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Intro — spoken, one voice and handclap]
They separated us by shade,
by hair, by body, by name,
then told us we were strangers.
Look around this room.
Every woman here is a sister.
Every hand here is a promise.
We are not the scattered pieces.
We are the whole.
Rise, Black women.
Rise.

[Verse 1 — spoken-sung, building]
I was taught to compete with the woman next to me,
to measure my worth by the mirror and the street.
They split us by shade, by hair, by class, by name,
then sold us the lie that we were never the same.
But I see her pain and it looks like mine,
the same fire in her eyes, the same steel in her spine.
She held me when I was falling, she never asked why,
she just opened her arms and let me cry.
So I'm done with the jealousy, done with the shame,
done with the contest, done with the game.
Every Black woman is a sister to me,
every shade, every body, every history.

[Pre-Chorus — call and response]
From the north, from the south,
from the east, from the west,
bring your broken heart,
bring your best.
Every language, every land,
every woman, every hand,
we are the love we were never given,
we are the home we build to live in.

[Chorus — 4x, all female]
Rise, Black women, rise
Stand up, stand up, stand up
Rise, Black women, rise
Every hand in every hand
Rise, Black women, rise
We are a militant army of love
Rise, Black women, rise
No sister stands alone

[Verse 2 — spoken-sung, sharper]
They burned the records and they buried the names,
then told us our story began with the chains.
They hid the queens in the margins, the scholars, the healers,
the women who built the world and the women who freed us.
They taught us to hate what we see in the glass,
to forget the empires that our mothers outlasted.
But the truth is a seed that survives the concrete,
it grows in the dark and it breaks through the street.
I'm pulling the curtain, I'm opening the door,
I'm naming the women they erased before.
The history they concealed is the weapon we hold,
and the army of truth is a sight to behold.

[Chorus — 4x, all female]
Rise, Black women, rise
Stand up, stand up, stand up
Rise, Black women, rise
Every hand in every hand
Rise, Black women, rise
We are a militant army of love
Rise, Black women, rise
No sister stands alone

[Bridge — tender, then rising]
Hold my hand, sister, hold my hand,
we don't have to carry this alone.
Hold my hand, sister, hold my hand,
we are the arms of a home.
Hug me like you know my name,
hug me like you know my pain,
hug me like the world is watching
and we're still standing in the rain.
We don't need permission to love each other out loud.
We don't need a rescue when we are the crowd.
So hold on, hold on, hold on,
the struggle is real, but the love is strong.

[Verse 3 — spoken-sung, militant]
We are a militant army, but our weapons are love,
our discipline is truth, our uniform is us.
We don't need a general to tell us where to stand,
every woman is a leader with a future in her hands.
We protect the young ones, we carry the old,
we share the load, we break the mold.
If one of us is bleeding, we all feel the cut,
if one of us is rising, we all lift her up.
No woman is a threat when her sister is free,
no woman is alone in this army of we.
So bring your voice, bring your hands, bring your heart,
and watch a million women tear the silence apart.

[Chorus — 4x, all female]
Rise, Black women, rise
Stand up, stand up, stand up
Rise, Black women, rise
Every hand in every hand
Rise, Black women, rise
We are a militant army of love
Rise, Black women, rise
No sister stands alone

[Outro — spoken, then full choir]
Rise, Black women, rise.
Not in spite of the struggle—
through it.
Holding hands.
Hugging hearts.
Telling the truth.
No woman left behind.
Rise.
Rise.
Rise.
