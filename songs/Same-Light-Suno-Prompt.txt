SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL SOUL BALLAD "SAME LIGHT"

STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box — 803 characters)
Warm soul ballad, 70 BPM, D major, straight 4/4, lazy steady backbeat. Felt
piano leads, Wurlitzer underneath, upright bass on long notes, brushed drums
with a rim click on two and four, nylon guitar repeating one figure. Low organ
pad from the first chorus, strings swell once at the bridge, close unpolished
voices humming under the last chorus. Warm mid-range male tenor, almost spoken
in the verses, lifting on the choruses, light rasp, no tuning sheen; a female
alto answers in the choruses and takes two lines of her own at the end, level
with him. Piano and guitar alone at the top, full room on the final chorus,
then back to felt piano and a spoken last line. Quiet analog mix, vocal
forward, soft low end. Sincere, grounded, never sugary. Original lyrics,
melody and arrangement; no imitation.

SHORT STYLE (use if the Style box rejects the long version — 323 characters)
Soul ballad, 70 BPM, D major, felt piano, Wurlitzer, upright bass, brushed

humming voices on the last chorus, warm male tenor with a female alto
answering, stripped bridge then full last chorus, quiet analog mix. Sincere,
