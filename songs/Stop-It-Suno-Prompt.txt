STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 723 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Slow, late-'90s Down South hardcore protest rap at 76 BPM in F minor, half-time so the 808 drags. Trunk-rattling sub-bass, lazy kick, sharp snares, tight triplet hi-hats, dark minor-key Rhodes, dirty wah guitar, low brass swells and vinyl crackle. Deep male lead, conversational and behind the beat, almost spoken in the verses, doubled low on the chant hook; a small male group answers in call-and-response, with a distant female hum under the bridge. Sparse arrangement with big empty space, one abrupt drop to sub-bass and voice before the final hook. Warm analog mix, low end forward, dry close vocal, no bright pop polish. Serious, defiant, heavy and cinematic. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
