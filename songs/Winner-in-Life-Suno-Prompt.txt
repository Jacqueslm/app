SUNO PROMPT — ORIGINAL TRIUMPHANT HIP-HOP "WINNER IN LIFE"
A beginner's trading story: heartbreak first, then discipline, process and victory.
Chants and ad-libs run through it; the seven-line "I am winner" chant starts over after each cycle.
Two separate prompts: Style box, then Lyrics box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 849 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Heartfelt triumphant hip-hop at 88 BPM in B minor. Starts with cracked piano, rain, a heartbroken spoken intro; verses in a fast tongue-twister pocket over soul-trap drums, 808, snare rolls and a warm vocal sample; the chorus drops to half-time with wide harmonies, brass, stadium drums and a crowd chant. A stadium-sized chorus with choir-like harmonies, brass swells and a crowd chant. Piano and strings under the last chorus, then a bare vocal tag. The last chorus adds strings, timpani and a crowd. Crowd chants and ad-libs throughout; a seven-line "I am winner" chant after the first and final choruses, then it starts over. Male lead rises from wounded to victorious; ad-libs and doubled vocals. The beat is a slow head-nod with fast verses, not trap. No auto-tune, no EDM, no pop polish. Original lyrics, melody, vocal cadence and drum pattern.
