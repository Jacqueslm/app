SUNO PROMPT - "YOU ARE NOT A SEWER"
Spoken-word soul-rap anthem. One voice, plain words, nothing borrowed from any
belief system. Written to be read aloud, not rapped fast.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE OF MUSIC (paste into the Style box - 716 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spoken-word soul-rap anthem at 88 BPM in D minor. One deep male voice, close-miked and calm, half spoken half sung, telling it plain like a man talking to a friend. The hook is answered by a warm uplifted choir of five singers in three-part harmony, handclaps, soft tambourine. Felt piano and Rhodes, warm Hammond organ, round upright bass, brushed drums that enter at the third verse, one clean electric guitar, low strings that swell under the last hook. Builds slowly: almost bare at the top, full and triumphant at the end, never shouting, never rushed. Intimate room sound, warm analog mix, space left around every word. Original words, melody and cadence; do not recreate or imitate any existing song or voice.

LYRICS (paste into the Lyrics box - 3184 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Intro - spoken, low and close, no drums]
Listen.
You did not come here broken.

[Verse 1 - spoken, the old story]
They told you your body is a sewer.
They told you your gut is a backed-up drain,
full of poison, full of slime,
full of waste that never left.
They told you, you must be cleaned out,
or the dirt will come and take you.
They told you, go after every plate.
Run to the toilet. Beat the clock.
Sit there and wait for it.
And if it does not come, you are behind.
You are dirty. You are late.
That is what they told you.

[Chorus - one voice and choir, warm]
You are not a sewer.
You are not a drain.
You are not a mess somebody left behind.
You are a house that is lived in.
Clean the steps. Open the windows.
Let the sun come in.

[Verse 2 - spoken, the answer, plain]
Listen.
That is a story. It is an old story.
It is not true.
Your liver is a cleaner. It works all day.
Your kidneys clean your blood. They never sleep.
Your lungs blow the bad air out,
every breath, and they do not ask you first.
You do not have to flush a house that cleans itself.
You just have to stop throwing mud in the door.

[Chorus - one voice and choir]
You are not a sewer.
You are not a drain.
You are not a mess somebody left behind.
You are a house that is lived in.
Clean the steps. Open the windows.
Let the sun come in.

[Verse 3 - spoken, the belly, drums enter]
And your belly?
Your belly has its own clock.
Some people go three times a day.
Some go three times a week.
Both are normal. Both are fine.
You do not have to run after every plate.
Nobody does.
Your body is not a timer for you to beat.

[Verse 4 - spoken, the plants]
And the plants?
The plants are real. The plants are strong.
Strong is not the same as soft.
A strong plant is a medicine.
A medicine has a size. A medicine has a danger.
Ask someone who knows
before you pour it in a cup and drink it every night.
Something that grows is not always something safe to drink forever.

[Chorus - one voice and choir]
You are not a sewer.
You are not a drain.
You are not a mess somebody left behind.
You are a house that is lived in.
Clean the steps. Open the windows.
Let the sun come in.

[Verse 5 - spoken, quiet, then hard]
And here is the one I need you to hear.
Fear does not heal you.
Fear sells you a bottle.
Fear says you are full of poison, so buy this.
Fear says you are behind, so hurry.
Fear says your body is the enemy.
Your body is not the enemy.

[Verse 6 - spoken, the small thing]
So here is the small thing. The real thing.
Eat food that grew. Not food that was made.
Drink water. Sleep in the dark.
Move your legs. Sweat a little. Breathe slow.
That is the whole magic.
That is the whole cleanse.

[Final chorus - bigger, choir and strings]
You are not a sewer.
You are not a drain.
You are not a punishment.
You are not a mess somebody left behind.
You are the first home you ever had,
and it has been keeping the lights on
for you the whole time.

[Bridge - quiet, choir underneath]
And when something is wrong,
go and get it looked at.
That is not weakness.
That is sense.

[Outro - spoken, soft, fading]
Clean the steps. Open the windows. Let the sun in.
And then go and live in it.
Today is day one.

WHAT THIS IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The speech as a record: six spoken verses, three choruses and a final chorus, a
quiet bridge and a spoken outro. The sewer talk is in it - verse one is the voice
that told you your body is a backed-up drain, and everything after it is the
answer. 88 BPM so every word lands.
