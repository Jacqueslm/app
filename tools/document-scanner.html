📄 Document Scanner & Converter

Scan or upload → read the text → edit it → save in the format you want. Everything happens on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1 — Add your document

Take a photo with your camera, or add photos and PDF files.

…or drag files here

Step 2 — Read the text (OCR)

This reads the words out of your photos and PDFs. It can take a minute for big files.

Handwriting mode sharpens the picture and reads it line by line. It does well with neat, separated printing. Joined-up cursive is still very hit-and-miss — expect to fix a lot in Step 3.

Step 3 — Edit the text

Fix anything the reader got wrong, or type your own text.

Step 4 — Save it

Pick a format. The file downloads straight to your device.
