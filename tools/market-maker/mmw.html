Market Maker Warfare

The briefing room. Seven lessons on ground you keep walking over — ten questions each, pass with 7. Clear all seven and the campaign opens: thirty operations, five ranks, and a war that never ends after that.

Campaign Map

Every operation is real tape — a stretch of MES, MNQ or gold that actually happened, with the name and the date held back until you are out. The market rotates, so the campaign walks you through all three. Pick the bar you want to trade: the tape underneath is the same hours either way, just grouped bigger, so the structure gets slower and wider — and the bridge above always steps up from it. Winning trades are rounds on target; stop-outs are rounds you take. Hit the objective before extraction. Stars are for taking no damage.
